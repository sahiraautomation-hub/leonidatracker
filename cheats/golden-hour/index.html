How to enter the Golden Hour code

  1. Press Up on the D-pad during gameplay to take out the phone.
  2. On PS5, hold L1 and press R1. On Xbox Series X|S, hold LB and press RB.
  3. Keep holding and press: Down, Down, Up, Right, Right, Up, Left, Left, Up, Up.
  4. Wait for the on-screen confirmation.

Ends Up, Up. Time accumulates across sessions, so walk hand in hand between nearby objectives.

How to unlock Golden Hour

Spend four hours in total holding hands with your partner. Like every cheat in GTA 6, it also stays locked until the main story is finished.

Keep count on the Hours holding hands with your partner counter in the Tracker. The target is 4.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Golden Hour cheat code in GTA 6?

Take out the phone, hold L1 and press R1 on PS5 (LB and RB on Xbox), then enter Down, Down, Up, Right, Right, Up, Left, Left, Up, Up.

How do I unlock Golden Hour?

Spend four hours in total holding hands with your partner. You also need to have finished the main story.

Is the Golden Hour code the same on PS5 and Xbox?

Yes. The D-pad sequence is identical. Only the shoulder buttons differ: L1 and R1 on PS5, LB and RB on Xbox Series X|S.
