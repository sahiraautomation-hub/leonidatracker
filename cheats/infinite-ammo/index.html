GTA 6 Infinite Ammo Cheat Code for PS5 and Xbox

How to enter the Infinite Ammo code

  1. Press Up on the D-pad during gameplay to take out the phone.
  2. On PS5, hold L1 and press R1. On Xbox Series X|S, hold LB and press RB.
  3. Keep holding and press: Down, Up, Right, Down, Left, Left, Up, Down, Down, Down.
  4. Wait for the on-screen confirmation.

Ends Down, Down, Down. Think of it as the bottom-heavy ammo code.

How to unlock Infinite Ammo

Earn 25 silver-or-better medals at shooting ranges. Like every cheat in GTA 6, it also stays locked until the main story is finished.

Keep count on the Shooting range medals, silver or better counter in the Tracker. The target is 25.

Codes that look almost the same

Infinite Ammo belongs to a family of codes that open the same way. Entering the wrong ending triggers a different cheat, or nothing at all.

  • Max Ammo shares the first 7 presses. It splits at press 8: Down for Infinite Ammo, Up for Max Ammo.
  • No Reload shares the first 9 presses. It splits at press 10: Down for Infinite Ammo, Up for No Reload.

Learn the shared opening once, then focus on the ending. The code patterns guide covers every family.

Before you use it

Using any cheat disables saving, trophies and achievements for that session. Make a manual save in a separate slot first, and reload your clean save when you are done. Details in cheats, trophies and saving. If the code does nothing, work through the seven common causes.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Infinite Ammo cheat code in GTA 6?

Take out the phone, hold L1 and press R1 on PS5 (LB and RB on Xbox), then enter Down, Up, Right, Down, Left, Left, Up, Down, Down, Down.

How do I unlock Infinite Ammo?

Earn 25 silver-or-better medals at shooting ranges. You also need to have finished the main story.

Is the Infinite Ammo code the same on PS5 and Xbox?

Yes. The D-pad sequence is identical. Only the shoulder buttons differ: L1 and R1 on PS5, LB and RB on Xbox Series X|S.
