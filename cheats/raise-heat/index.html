GTA 6 Raise Heat Level Cheat Code for PS5 and Xbox

Raise Heat Level is one of GTA 6's player cheats. Raises your heat level across the map. Below is the exact input for both consoles, what you need to do to unlock it, and how to avoid the common mistakes.

How to enter the Raise Heat Level code

  1. Press Up on the D-pad during gameplay to take out the phone.
  2. On PS5, hold L1 and press R1. On Xbox Series X|S, hold LB and press RB.
  3. Keep holding and press: Left, Down, Right, Down, Up, Down, Up.
  4. Wait for the on-screen confirmation.

Only the sixth input differs from Raise Wanted Level (Down here, Right there). Double-check before you enter it.

How to unlock Raise Heat Level

Lose a three-star wanted level 25 times. Like every cheat in GTA 6, it also stays locked until the main story is finished.

Keep count on the Three-star wanted levels lost counter in the Tracker. The target is 25. The same counter also unlocks Raise Wanted Level at 50.

Codes that look almost the same

Raise Heat Level belongs to a family of codes that open the same way. Entering the wrong ending triggers a different cheat, or nothing at all.

  • Raise Wanted Level shares the first 5 presses. It splits at press 6: Down for Raise Heat Level, Right for Raise Wanted Level.

Learn the shared opening once, then focus on the ending. The code patterns guide covers every family.

Before you use it

Using any cheat disables saving, trophies and achievements for that session. Make a manual save in a separate slot first, and reload your clean save when you are done. Details in cheats, trophies and saving. If the code does nothing, work through the seven common causes.

Frequently asked questions

What is the Raise Heat Level cheat code in GTA 6?

Take out the phone, hold L1 and press R1 on PS5 (LB and RB on Xbox), then enter Left, Down, Right, Down, Up, Down, Up.

How do I unlock Raise Heat Level?

Lose a three-star wanted level 25 times. You also need to have finished the main story.

Is the Raise Heat Level code the same on PS5 and Xbox?

Yes. The D-pad sequence is identical. Only the shoulder buttons differ: L1 and R1 on PS5, LB and RB on Xbox Series X|S.
