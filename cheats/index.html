GTA 6 Cheats for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S

All 61 cheats in GTA 6, grouped by type. All 61 codes are listed, and every cheat lists the challenge that unlocks it. Switch platform to see the right shoulder buttons. Last verified 2026-11-28.

How to enter a cheat

  1. Press Up on the D-pad during gameplay to bring out the phone.
  2. Hold L1 and press R1.Hold LB and press RB.
  3. Keep holding and enter the D-pad arrows.
  4. Look for the confirmation message on screen.

Rules to know first

  • Cheats only appear after you finish the main story. Before that, the Cheats list in the Progress menu stays locked.
  • Every cheat also has its own unlock challenge, listed next to it below.
  • Using a cheat disables saving, trophies and achievements for that session, so make a separate save first.
  • Cheats are a Story Mode feature on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The D-pad sequence is the same on both consoles.

Frequently asked questions

How do I enter cheats in GTA 6?

Press Up on the D-pad to take out the phone, hold L1 and press R1 on PS5 (LB and RB on Xbox), then enter the D-pad sequence. See the full input guide.

Why does my code not work?

The cheat has to be unlocked first: finish the main story, then complete that cheat's own challenge. Also keep both shoulder buttons held for the whole sequence.

Do cheats disable trophies?

Yes. Cheats disable saving, trophies and achievements for that session. Keep a separate save. More in our trophies guide.

When will the missing codes be added?

As soon as a code is confirmed by more than one independent source. Each missing entry already lists the challenge that unlocks it.
