GTA 6 Partner Cheats: Dollhouse, Faded Glory, Golden Hour and More

CheatsBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

GTA 6 is built around a partnership, and five of its cheats reward time spent together. Planned well, a single stretch of partner play can move all five counters.

The five partner unlocks

  • Golden Hour: four hours in total holding hands.
  • Dollhouse: eight hours with your partner.
  • Videotape: 50 activities with your partner.
  • Pinhole: 25 gang operations cleared together.
  • Faded Glory: 50 stores robbed together.

Stacking them

Time with your partner builds up whenever you play together, so taking your partner on robberies, gang operations and activities moves several of these counters at once. Holding hands is separate. Walk hand in hand between nearby objectives instead of driving short distances, and Golden Hour fills up over time.

Cheat-by-cheat notes

Golden Hour

Applies the Golden Hour screen filter. To unlock it, spend four hours in total holding hands with your partner. The input is 10 presses: Down, Down, Up, Right, Right, Up, Left, Left, Up, Up. It shares its first 8 or more inputs with Pinhole, Cataclysm, Videotape, Dark Mirror, Dollhouse, Faded Glory, so slow down at the end. Ends Up, Up. Time accumulates across sessions, so walk hand in hand between nearby objectives.

Dollhouse

Applies a vivid filter with a narrow aspect ratio. To unlock it, spend eight hours with your partner. The input is 10 presses: Down, Down, Up, Right, Right, Up, Left, Left, Down, Right. It shares its first 8 or more inputs with Golden Hour, Pinhole, Cataclysm, Videotape, Dark Mirror, Faded Glory, so slow down at the end. Ends Down, Right. Cataclysm ends Right, Right.

Videotape

Applies a videotape-style filter. To unlock it, complete 50 activities with your partner. The input is 10 presses: Down, Down, Up, Right, Right, Up, Left, Left, Right, Down. It shares its first 8 or more inputs with Golden Hour, Pinhole, Cataclysm, Dark Mirror, Dollhouse, Faded Glory, so slow down at the end. Partner activities stack with Dollhouse time, so plan them together.

Pinhole

Applies the Pinhole screen filter. To unlock it, clear 25 gang operations with your partner. The input is 10 presses: Down, Down, Up, Right, Right, Up, Left, Left, Up, Down. It shares its first 8 or more inputs with Golden Hour, Cataclysm, Videotape, Dark Mirror, Dollhouse, Faded Glory, so slow down at the end. Ends Up, Down. Operations cleared solo do not count, so bring your partner.

Faded Glory

Applies a colour CRT TV filter. To unlock it, rob 50 stores with your partner. The input is 10 presses: Down, Down, Up, Right, Right, Up, Left, Left, Left, Right. It shares its first 8 or more inputs with Golden Hour, Pinhole, Cataclysm, Videotape, Dark Mirror, Dollhouse, so slow down at the end. Ends Left, Right. The three found filter codes differ only in their final two inputs.

Frequently asked questions

Do solo store robberies count for Faded Glory?

No. The requirement is 50 stores robbed with your partner.

Which partner cheat has a known code?

Dollhouse, Faded Glory and Videotape.

Does time holding hands count toward Dollhouse?

They are listed as separate challenges, and overlap has not been confirmed. Track both counters on their own.
