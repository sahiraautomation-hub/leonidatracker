GTA 6 Trophy and Achievement List: All 55 and How to Earn Them

ProgressBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

GTA 6 has 55 trophies on PS5: one Platinum, two Gold, six Silver and 46 Bronze. On Xbox the same list is worth 1,000 Gamerscore. Twenty-seven are visible from the start and 28 are hidden, most of them tied to the story. This page lists every visible trophy and describes the hidden ones without spoiling the plot.

These are shown in the trophy list from the moment you start the game.

The 28 hidden trophies, without spoilers

  • Twelve are story trophies. They unlock as you finish chapters and major missions. One of them, for the final mission, is the second Gold. You cannot miss these by playing the story.
  • Six are for side characters. Finishing the strands for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone each gives a trophy, and Bret has one for a special hunt. See Strangers and Freaks.
  • The rest are one-off actions. Confirmed examples include maxing Strength for both characters (Personal Best), performing 500 actions that change your Criminal Profile (Actions Speak Louder), using a tank (Challenging Tank) and taking off from the airport unannounced (Catch Me If You Can). The triggers for five others are still unconfirmed.

The one trophy you can miss

Positive Mental Attitude asks you to meditate at every spot in Leonida. One meditation, with Lucia on the balcony of Jason's house, is only available in Chapter 1. Guides have not confirmed whether it is strictly required, but none have ruled it out, so do it before the chapter ends. The full list of time-limited content is in the missables guide.

A spoiler-light roadmap

  1. Chapter 1: do Lucia's balcony meditation, and keep a manual save.
  2. From Chapter 2: work on the partner trophies, Hot Together and Retail Therapy. Buy garages for Little Boxes once the Real Estate app arrives.
  3. Chapter 3: get the Snap Gun for Sneakthief, and do the time-limited street race while it is on offer.
  4. Chapter 4: take the optional mission that opens bank vaults, for Maximum Withdrawal.
  5. After the credits: finish the 100% checklist, the side-character strands and the long grinds such as 250 stunts.

There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so keep a save at the start of each chapter. See how to save.

Never use cheats on your trophy save

Using any cheat disables trophies and saving for that session. Cheats only unlock after the story anyway, so by then most trophies are behind you. Keep a separate slot for cheating. See cheats, trophies and saving.

Frequently asked questions

How many trophies are in GTA 6?

55: one Platinum, two Gold, six Silver and 46 Bronze. On Xbox the achievements total 1,000 Gamerscore.

Are there missable trophies in GTA 6?

Possibly one. Positive Mental Attitude may need a meditation with Lucia that is only available in Chapter 1, so do it before the chapter ends.
