Randy's Wish List in GTA 6: How to Unlock It and Get Paid

VehiclesBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

Quick answer: Randy's Wish List unlocks about halfway through Chapter 3, when a mission for Randy gives you the Key Cloner. Steal the high-end cars on his list and deliver them undamaged to his garage in western Vice City for a reported $30,000 or more each. It is the best-paying car job in the game.

How to unlock it

The Wish List opens about halfway through Chapter 3. A mission for Randy has you steal a supercar for him, and it gives you the Key Cloner. After that mission, the list is available and Randy's garage in western Vice City accepts deliveries.

What the Key Cloner does

High-end cars cannot be taken like ordinary ones. The Key Cloner is the tool that gets you into them. You keep it after the mission, so it is useful well beyond Randy's list. Any supercar you want for yourself needs it too.

How deliveries work

  1. Check the list for a car Randy still needs. The most detailed guides name 20 cars. See every car and where to find it.
  2. Find one. Most drive around Vice City, and some parked examples are marked on your map.
  3. Steal it with the Key Cloner.
  4. Drive carefully. Randy pays full price only for a car in perfect condition.
  5. Deliver it to his garage.

Each car is reported to pay $30,000 or more. Damage cuts the price, so a slow, clean drive beats a fast one.

Rewards

  • Supply & Demand (Bronze trophy): awarded for finishing Randy's strand. Guides report it arrives after 16 deliveries.
  • Randy's deliveries are also reported to be part of the 100% checklist.
  • Finishing the full list is reported to reward the Truffade Thrax, the fastest car in the game.

Tips

Lose the police before you head to the garage. Arriving with a wanted level risks the car and the payout. Avoid motorways at speed, since one heavy crash can ruin a delivery. And scan before you steal: the WAiNK app shows whether a car has a tracker.

Frequently asked questions

How do you unlock Randy's Wish List in GTA 6?

Complete Randy's mission midway through Chapter 3. It gives you the Key Cloner and opens the list.

How much does Randy pay per car?

Launch guides report $30,000 or more for each car delivered in perfect condition.

What is the Key Cloner?

A tool that lets you steal high-end cars. You receive it during Randy's mission in Chapter 3.
