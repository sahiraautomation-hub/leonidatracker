How Many Missions Are in GTA 6?

MissionsBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

GTA 6's main story has 91 missions split across five chapters, following Jason and Lucia from the Leonida Keys to Vice City and beyond.

The five chapters

  • Chapter 1: Just Like Paradise (22 missions, Leonida Keys)
  • Chapter 2: Paradise Lost (11 missions, Port Gellhorn)
  • Chapter 3 (26 missions), where the story reaches Vice City
  • Chapter 4: Vice City, Baby (25 missions)
  • Chapter 5: Top of the World, Ma (7 missions)

That adds up to 91. Chapters 1 and 2 together account for 33 missions, and Chapters 3 and 4 hold more than half of the story with 51 between them.

Missions and cheats

Finishing all 91 is what unlocks the Cheats list. Four cheats become available immediately: $250,000, Unlimited Focus, Attack Force and Cycle Weather.

Tracking your story

The Tracker lists Chapters 1 and 2 mission by mission, with names hidden until you tap them, plus a single milestone for finishing the story.

Chapter 1 unlocks worth knowing

Several core systems arrive through early story missions. Gear Up opens gun shops and loadout management. Just a Scratch unlocks the Brian's Lockup drop-off. Unwanted Attention adds Pay 'n' Spray shops and the cop scanner, and Buck Me sets up your bank account. The Chapter 1 guide lists all 22 missions in order.

How far along are you?

With 91 missions in total, finishing Chapter 1 puts you about 24% through the main story (22 of 91), and finishing Chapter 2 about 36% (33 of 91). Chapter 3 takes you to 59 of 91 (65%) and Chapter 4 to 84 of 91 (92%), leaving a short seven-mission final chapter.

Why later chapters are not listed mission by mission

Mission names past Chapter 2 give away major story beats, and early community lists still disagree on their order. Rather than publish a list that might be wrong, the Tracker uses a single milestone for finishing the story. That is also the checkbox that unlocks the cheat rows in the Cheat Unlock Planner.
