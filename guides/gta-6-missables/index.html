GTA 6 Missables: What You Can Miss and When to Do It

ProgressBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

GTA 6 has no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so anything you miss stays missed unless you have an older save. The list of true missables is short, and most of it sits in Chapter 1. Here is everything to do before each point of no return, with no story spoilers.

Before the end of Chapter 1

  • Get Shirley's Rum and Steal the Scooter: two optional errands for Jason's neighbour, Shirley. Both are gone after Chapter 1.
  • Meditate with Lucia: an optional meditation on the balcony of Jason's house, available after the mission Down to Earth. It may be needed for the Positive Mental Attitude trophy, so do not skip it.
  • Empty the floorboard stash. Cash left under Jason's floor is reported not to carry into Chapter 2. Bank it or spend it.
  • Use edition bonuses. Ultimate Edition owners report that some bonus items, including a bike, a kayak and a custom build for Jason's car, are only usable in Chapter 1.
  • Make a manual save in a slot you will not overwrite.

The mission Tourist Trap is the point of no return for Chapter 1. Do these first.

During Chapter 3

  • Yago's timed race. One of Yago's street races is time-limited and marked with an hourglass. Do it when it appears. Yago's strand counts toward the Strangers & Freaks trophy.
  • Errands with an hourglass icon in general are reported to expire if you leave them, so treat that icon as a warning.

During Chapter 4

  • The optional vault mission. Bank vaults are opened by an optional mission near the end of Chapter 4. You need vaults for the Maximum Withdrawal trophy, so start it before you move on. See bank vault robberies.

Permanent choices

  • Irredeemable Criminal Profile. The worst profile level is permanent for that character. Save before any rampage. See Criminal Profile.
  • Your first free respray. When Joey returns Jason's repaired car after Just a Scratch, players report that the colour you choose is the only free respray, so pick one you like.

Things that are not missable

Story trophies cannot be missed. Collectibles such as cards, Cocaine Flamingos, WAiNK scans and underwater discoveries can be collected after the credits. Challenges and cheat unlocks also stay open. If something is not on this page, you can almost certainly come back for it.

Your safety net

Keep one save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. That routine, described in how to save, turns every item on this list from a permanent loss into a minor detour. You can tick off chapter missions as you go in the Tracker.

Frequently asked questions

What is missable in GTA 6 Chapter 1?

Shirley's two errands, the balcony meditation with Lucia, and any cash left in the floorboard stash. Do them before the mission Tourist Trap.

Is there a point of no return in GTA 6?

Yes. Tourist Trap is the point of no return for Chapter 1.

Can you replay missions to get missed content?

No. GTA 6 has no mission replay or New Game Plus, so use manual saves.
