GTA 6 Progress Menu: What Each of the 12 Cards Tracks

ProgressBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

The Progress tab is where GTA 6 keeps score. It holds your collectibles, challenges and, once the story is done, your cheats.

Opening it

Pause the game, open the Map and switch to the Progress tab. On PS5, highlight a card and press Cross to open it.

The 12 cards

  • Stats
  • Vehicles
  • Weapons
  • Animals
  • Equipment
  • Collections
  • Activities
  • Contacts
  • Gangs
  • Robberies
  • Challenges
  • Story Recap

Collections is the sixth card and holds card sets, WAiNK scans and exploration entries. Challenges holds the eight tracks that unlock vehicle spawn cheats. After the credits, the Cheats list also becomes available from the Progress menu.

Mirroring it here

The Tracker follows the same structure for collectibles and challenges, so you can plan between sessions on your phone or laptop without pausing the game.

Reading the Challenges card

Challenges are grouped into eight tracks: survival, combat, fitness, shopping, exploration, vehicle, sports and criminal. Every track pays out twice. Five completed challenges unlock one vehicle spawn cheat, and finishing the whole track unlocks a second. For example, five fitness challenges give the Tuff BMX and the full fitness track gives the '65 Vapid Dominator. Completing every challenge in the game unlocks the Hauners P-996 Lazer.

Because the five-challenge rewards come early, the Challenges card is the best place to look when you want a quick cheat unlock after the credits.

How Collections maps to 100%

Collections is where most 100% requirements live. Card sets, WAiNK scans, underwater discoveries and reading material all appear here with running totals. Not every list needs to be finished: 15 of 18 card sets and 30 of 34 reads are enough, while all 33 underwater discoveries are required. Copy the numbers from Collections into the Tracker at the end of a session and the 100% gap check will show what is left.
