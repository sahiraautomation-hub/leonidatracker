GTA 6 Screen Filter Cheats: All 16 Filters and How to Unlock Them

CheatsBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

Sixteen GTA 6 cheats change how the world looks, from a CRT television effect to a world full of smoke. They are perfect for photo mode, clips and a change of mood on a long completion run.

The seven known filter codes

Golden Hour, Pinhole, Cataclysm, Videotape, Dark Mirror, Dollhouse and Faded Glory are the filter codes found so far. All start Down, Down, Up, Right, Right, Up, Left, Left and then split on the last two presses. Golden Hour ends Up, Up. Pinhole ends Up, Down. Cataclysm ends Right, Right. Videotape ends Right, Down. Dark Mirror ends Right, Left. Dollhouse ends Down, Right. Faded Glory ends Left, Right. The Videotape and Dark Mirror codes were added on 23 November 2026, the Pinhole code on 25 November 2026 and the Golden Hour code on 26 November 2026.

Partner-based filters

Five filters need time or activities with your partner: Golden Hour (four hours holding hands), Dollhouse (eight hours together), Videotape (50 partner activities), Pinhole (25 gang operations together) and Faded Glory (50 store robberies together). See the partner cheats guide for how to stack them.

Collection and grind filters

The rest come from other systems. Bubblegum needs all 24 Vice City Flamingos cards. Neon Jungle needs all 16 Girlypop Vehicles. Fisheye needs 100 fish, Fast Forward 50 meditations, Hellscape 100 stolen police vehicles, and Migraine six minutes survived at a six-star wanted level. Dark Mirror and Pixel Art are money-based, at $500,000 from vehicle sales and $250,000 from collectible sales.

Frequently asked questions

Can I stack two filter cheats?

That has not been confirmed. Try it on a backup save, since cheats disable saving.

Which filter unlocks fastest?

Yesteryear and Cataclysm are single discoveries, so they can arrive early during exploration.

Do filters show up in screenshots?

They change the game image, so they appear in anything you capture.
