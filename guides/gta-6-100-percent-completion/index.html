GTA 6 100% Completion Checklist

ProgressBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

Reaching 100% in GTA 6 means finishing the story and then working through the Collections and Activities pages of the Progress menu. Some lists need every entry, others leave you some slack.

Where to check

Pause, open the Map, switch to the Progress tab and choose Collections. The Progress tab holds 12 cards in total; see the Progress menu guide for what each one shows.

Known requirements

  • Finish the main story first. 100% tracking opens after it.
  • Complete 15 of the 18 collectible card sets.
  • Find all 33 underwater discoveries.
  • Find 30 of the 34 magazines, books, comics and book swaps.
  • Destroy 100 of the 125 Cocaine Flamingos (community-reported).

Reaching 100% earns the A Long Road achievement.

A sensible order

Do the strict lists first, since you cannot skip any of them. Underwater discoveries come first. Then finish the smallest card sets and leave the 36-card sets for last. Finally, sweep flamingos and reads while exploring each region. The Tracker and the 100% gap calculator show exactly what is left.

Trophies and missable content

100% completion earns the Gold trophy A Long Road, one of 55 in the GTA 6 trophy list. A small amount of content can be locked out for good, mostly in Chapter 1, so check the missables guide before you move on.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need every collectible for 100% in GTA 6?

No. Several lists leave slack, such as 15 of 18 card sets. Underwater discoveries are an exception: all 33 are needed.

Can I start 100% before the story ends?

You can collect along the way, but 100% tracking opens after the story.

What is the 100% achievement called?

A Long Road.
