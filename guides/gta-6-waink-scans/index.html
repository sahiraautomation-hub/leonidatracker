GTA 6 WAiNK Scans: Landmarks, Curiosities and Sculptures

CollectiblesBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

WAiNK is a phone app in GTA 6 that turns sightseeing into a collectible. The Collections page splits scans into three categories: Landmarks, Curiosities and Sculptures.

The counts

  • 31 Landmarks
  • 35 Curiosities
  • 23 Sculptures

That is 89 scans across the three categories as listed in the Collections page.

Space debris and Powdered Sugar

Scanning all space debris with WAiNK unlocks the Powdered Sugar screen filter cheat. Keep the app in mind on long drives through open country.

Route planning

Scans are spread across Leonida, so tackle one region at a time and tick each region off in the Tracker once you have swept it.

Keeping three counts in sync

The Collections page shows Landmarks, Curiosities and Sculptures separately, so the Tracker does the same. Matching the in-game split means a mismatch is easy to spot. If your Tracker says 30 landmarks but the game says 29, you know exactly which list to recheck.

Combining scans with exploration

WAiNK scanning fits naturally into region sweeps. While you are in an area for other collectibles, keep the app in mind for anything that looks like a landmark, an oddity or a piece of public art. Space debris for Powdered Sugar is a separate goal, so it is worth noting those finds too.
