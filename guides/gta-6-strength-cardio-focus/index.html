GTA 6 Stats Guide: How to Increase Strength, Cardio and Focus

BasicsBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

Jason and Lucia each have three stats you train yourself: Strength, Cardio and Focus. They are not cosmetic. Strength sets your maximum health, Cardio sets your stamina, and Focus decides how long you can slow time in a fight.

The three stats

Each character trains separately, so progress on Jason does nothing for Lucia.

How to raise Strength

Strength comes from weight training. In Chapter 1 there are free weights on the terrace of Jason's house, so you can start without paying. Later, fitness studios around Leonida offer more equipment for a membership fee. Treadmills and bikes do not raise Strength. Stick to weights.

How to raise Cardio

Cardio is the easiest stat to build because ordinary travel counts. Running, swimming, cycling and kayaking all train it, as do treadmills, exercise bikes and the punching bag. Cycling somewhere instead of driving is free training.

How to raise Focus

Focus grows from calm, skilled activities instead of workouts: meditation, fishing, basketball, golf, pool and shooting-range challenges. Unlike the other two, Focus does not fade once you have raised it, so every gain is permanent.

Workout windows and recovery

  • Open the Fitness app on your phone to see your progress and your workout windows. Train while a window is open.
  • Finish the whole workout. Quitting early loses the gains from that session.
  • Eat protein afterwards. A protein shake from the fridge, or a bar at a studio, speeds up recovery.
  • Do not skip too many days. Strength and Cardio decline if you neglect them.
  • Train together. When Jason and Lucia work out at a studio as a pair, the character you are not controlling trains too.

Stats, trophies and cheats

  • Personal Best (hidden Bronze trophy): max out Strength for both characters.
  • Body Armor cheat: complete 100 workouts. See the Body Armor guide.
  • Fisheye and Fast Forward cheats: catch 100 fish and complete 50 meditations. Both also build Focus. See fishing and meditation.
  • After the story, the Unlimited Focus cheat removes the Focus limit entirely.

Frequently asked questions

How do you increase health in GTA 6?

Raise your Strength stat with weight training. Strength sets your maximum health.

Where can I work out in GTA 6?

On the terrace of Jason's house in Chapter 1 for free, and at fitness studios for a membership fee.

Does Focus go down in GTA 6?

No. Strength and Cardio fade if you stop training, but Focus gains are kept.
