GTA 6 Gun Shops: How to Buy, Unlock and Keep Your Weapons

WeaponsBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

Buying a gun in GTA 6 is simple. Keeping your guns is where players get caught out. Weapons you pick up from enemies do not belong to you, and they vanish when you die. This guide covers where to shop, how more stock unlocks, and how to hold on to what you have.

When gun shops unlock

Gun shops open with the Chapter 1 mission Gear Up, which sends Jason to buy a long gun. Buy one during the mission. Two guns are half price at that point, and skipping the purchase is reported to stall the next story call.

The shops

Guns are sold through Ammu-Nation distributors under local shop names. Three you will see early are Chuck's Tackle & Shot in the Keys, Big Shots in Hamlet and Iron & Oak. Stock differs between them. Chuck's and Big Shots carry the Duke 556 and the Shrewsbury Drifter, while Iron & Oak and Big Shots carry pistols, submachine guns and the Scorpio Akulus. Larger Ammu-Nation stores add sniper rifles and launchers.

How new guns unlock

Not everything is on the shelf at once. Part of the catalogue unlocks as you buy more weapons. Some guns are reported to appear after 8 further purchases and others after 13, including the best sniper rifle. Buying cheap guns you do not plan to use is one way to open the better ones sooner.

Owned guns and found guns

This is the rule that matters most. A gun you buy is owned. If you die or get arrested, it can be recovered. A gun you pick up from an enemy is not owned, and it is lost when you die or are busted.

To hold on to a found gun, store it in the trunk of your personal vehicle. The trunk also holds spare outfits, which helps when you need to change clothes to shake the police.

Carrying and holstering

You are reported to be limited to two sidearms and two long guns at a time, so choose your loadout before a job. Tap L1 on PS5 or LB on Xbox to holster or draw, and hold it to open the loadout wheel. Keep weapons holstered in public. Witnesses who see a drawn gun call the police, and a visible weapon is one of the things officers use to identify you.

Attachments and melee

Most guns can be modified with attachments. A few cannot, including the Shrewsbury Drifter. Melee weapons are cheap. Brass knuckles are listed at $95 and knives at around $300. For recommendations on what to buy, see the best weapons guide.

Frequently asked questions

When can you buy guns in GTA 6?

From the Chapter 1 mission Gear Up, which unlocks gun shops.

Do you lose your guns when you die in GTA 6?

Guns you bought can be recovered. Guns you picked up from enemies are lost unless you stored them in your vehicle's trunk.

How do I unlock more guns in the shop?

Buy more weapons. Parts of the catalogue are reported to unlock after 8 and 13 further purchases.
