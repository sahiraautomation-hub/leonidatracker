GTA 6 Characters: Who Is Who in Leonida

BasicsBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·4 min read

Quick answer: you play as Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, a couple trying to build a better life in the state of Leonida. Around them are Brian Heder and his crew in the Keys, a new set of faces in Chapter 2, and Vice City names such as Boobie Ike, Dre'Quan Priest, Real Dimez and Raul Bautista. This page says who each person is and where you meet them. It stops at introductions, so there are no twists, endings or late-game names here.

Jason and Lucia

Jason Duval grew up around small-time crooks, spent time in the Army and ended up in the Leonida Keys working for local drug runners. When the story opens he lives in a house that belongs to his boss, Brian.

Lucia Caminos begins the story in prison, where she landed while trying to look after her family. Getting out is her fresh start, and she wants more from life than scraping by.

The two are partners in work and in life. Chapter 1 is reported to be mostly Jason's, and from Chapter 2 you can switch between them. Each has separate stats, and some side missions belong to only one of them.

Chapter 1: Brian's circle in the Keys

  • Brian Heder. A veteran drug runner who moves his product through a boat yard in the Keys. He is Jason's landlord and gives him work.
  • Lori Heder. Brian's third wife, who runs the boat yard with him.
  • Cal Hampton. Jason's friend, who also works for Brian. He is the one to see about fishing.
  • Donnie. A pilot who flies drops for Brian.
  • Kenton Rees. A treasure hunter who sends Jason diving on wrecks. His dives open after the mission Island Dreams.
  • Andy. Lucia's close friend, who has been keeping her clothes while she was away. Andy has a Lucia-only side strand later on.
  • Mattia and Kat. Kat shared a cell with Lucia, and Mattia is her wealthy boyfriend. They arrive with an offer of work.
  • Joey. The mechanic at Coastal Auto Care. He fixes Jason's car in the mission Just a Scratch and can bring it back to you during Chapter 1.
  • Shirley Baker. Jason's neighbour. Her two short errands are only available in Chapter 1, as the Chapter 1 checklist explains.
  • Mandy. One of Brian's tenants.

Chapter 2: new faces

  • Vicki. A dancer who lives at the same motel.
  • Dunk. A local contact who helps Lucia get hold of painkillers.
  • Moses "Mo" Watkins. A mechanic in Port Gellhorn who buys stolen cars and makes it possible to register cars of your own. See selling cars and the best cars to sell to Moses.
  • Billy. Leads a dirt bike crew at the Thrillbilly Mud Club and has five optional missions.
  • Bret Vogel. A hunting contact for Jason. One character guide describes him as an old Army friend.

You meet all five in Chapter 2, while Jason and Lucia are living out of a motel.

Chapter 3 onwards: the names Rockstar introduced

  • Boobie Ike. A Vice City businessman with money in property, a strip club and a recording studio.
  • Dre'Quan Priest. A former dealer turned music executive. He runs the label Only Raw Records with Boobie.
  • Real Dimez. The rap duo Bae-Luxe and Roxy, old friends who are signed to Only Raw Records.
  • Raul Bautista. An experienced bank robber who is always looking for new people for bigger jobs.

Rockstar introduced all four on its own website before launch, so their set-up is not a spoiler. Guides place the move to Vice City in Chapter 3.

Vice City: dealers, buyers and racers

  • Randy Kozlov. A car dealer who buys stolen high-end cars. His list of wanted cars is covered in Randy's Wish List and Randy's cars.
  • Tyrone Russo. A dealer with a Jason-only side strand.
  • Yago Jiménez. A street racer who brings racing missions.

Kirk, Kyle McKittrick, Pilot Pete and the other side characters have short optional strands of their own. Strangers and Freaks lists them, who can play each one and which trophies they feed.

What this page leaves out

Characters who first appear in Chapters 4 and 5 are left out. So are the story's villains and any faces from earlier GTA games. With those, even a name gives something away.

This page also does not list voice actors. The full cast is on Rockstar's credits page, and one outlet's copy of that list is reported to run to about 200 named roles. If you want the story beats in order, the Chapter 1 and Chapter 2 mission lists are the place to start.

Frequently asked questions

Who are the main characters in GTA 6?

Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos. Both are playable, and you can switch between them from Chapter 2.

Who is Randy in GTA 6?

Randy Kozlov is a Vice City car dealer who buys stolen high-end cars. His Wish List opens in Chapter 3.

Who is Brian in GTA 6?

Brian Heder is a long-time drug runner in the Keys. He is Jason's landlord and gives him work in Chapter 1.

Who are Real Dimez in GTA 6?

A rap duo, Bae-Luxe and Roxy, signed to Only Raw Records in Vice City.

Does this page have spoilers?

No. It covers who each character is when you first meet them and leaves out the later chapters.
