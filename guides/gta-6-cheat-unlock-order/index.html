The Best Order to Unlock GTA 6 Cheats

CheatsBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·3 min read

Many GTA 6 cheat challenges share a counter or feed each other. If you plan around that overlap, one stretch of play unlocks two or three cheats instead of one. This order is built from the unlock requirements themselves.

Step 1: finish the story

Nothing unlocks before the credits, so the story comes first. It immediately gives you $250,000, Unlimited Focus, Attack Force and Cycle Weather.

Step 2: explosives and fire

Explosive damage has a two-stage counter. Twenty enemies hit unlocks Exploding Balls, and 100 unlocks the Explosive Shotgun. While you are at it, throw grenades (100 for All Explosive Weapons) and Molotovs (100 for Exploding Bottles). Molotovs also burn enemies, which pushes the 100 fire-damage counter for Incendiary Ammo. Blowing up vehicles in the same sessions counts toward the 100 needed for the Hauners H-AT. That is up to six cheats from one style of play.

Step 3: wanted levels

Escaping a three-star wanted level counts twice: 25 escapes for Raise Heat Level and 50 for Raise Wanted Level. Steal police vehicles during these chases and you also work toward the 100 needed for Hellscape. Vehicle kills during chases count toward the 25 for Vehicle Force.

Step 4: shooting ranges

Aim for gold from the start. A gold medal also counts as silver-or-better and bronze-or-better, so 25 golds unlock Max Ammo, Infinite Ammo and No Reload together. All three codes are known.

Step 5: partner time

Take your partner on store robberies (50 for Faded Glory), gang operations (25 for Pinhole) and activities (50 for Videotape). Time together builds toward Dollhouse at eight hours, and walking hand in hand builds Golden Hour at four.

Step 6: collectibles with two payoffs

Watches unlock Move Time Forward at 10 and Pause Time of Day at 20. Cocaine Flamingos unlock the Inflatable Flamingo at 50 and count toward 100% at 100. The Vice City Flamingos card set unlocks Bubblegum and counts as one of the 15 sets needed for 100%.

Step 7: challenge tracks

Each of the eight tracks gives a vehicle at five challenges and a second for the full track. Take every track to five first for eight quick vehicles, then finish tracks one at a time. Completing all of them unlocks the Hauners P-996 Lazer.

Item-by-item notes

Enemies hit with explosive damage

The full count is 100. 20 for Exploding Balls, 100 for the Explosive Shotgun. On this counter: Exploding Balls unlocks at 20; Explosive Shotgun (Mason & Keen Shortstraw) unlocks at 100.

Three-star wanted levels lost

The full count is 50. 25 for Raise Heat Level, 50 for Raise Wanted Level. On this counter: Raise Heat Level unlocks at 25; Raise Wanted Level unlocks at 50.

Collectible watches

The full count is 20. 10 unlocks Move Time Forward, 20 unlocks Pause Time of Day. On this counter: Move Time Forward unlocks at 10; Pause Time of Day unlocks at 20.

Cocaine Flamingos destroyed

The full count is 125. Shoot them to destroy. 50 unlocks the Inflatable Flamingo cheat. On this counter: Inflatable Flamingo unlocks at 50. For 100% completion: 100 of 125. Community-reported, not yet confirmed by Rockstar.

Shooting range gold medals

The full count is 25. No Reload cheat. On this counter: No Reload unlocks at 25.

Frequently asked questions

Which GTA 6 challenges unlock more than one cheat?

Explosive hits (20 and 100), three-star escapes (25 and 50), collectible watches (10 and 20) and each challenge track (five and all).

Do gold medals count toward all three ammo cheats?

Yes. Max Ammo accepts bronze or better and Infinite Ammo silver or better, so gold counts for all three.

Can I work on challenges before finishing the story?

You can build the totals as you play, but the cheats themselves only become available after the story.
