GTA 6 vs GTA 5 Cheats: What Changed and What Stayed the Same

CheatsBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·3 min read

If you played GTA 5, you remember typing in a button combo and getting a helicopter on the spot. GTA 6 keeps cheats but changes how you earn and enter them. Here is what returning players need to know.

Cheats must be earned now

In GTA 5, cheats worked from the first minute. You only needed to know the code. In GTA 6, every cheat is locked until the main story is complete, and all but four also have their own challenge. Body Armor asks for 100 workouts, and the ammo cheats ask for shooting-range medals. Knowing a code is no longer enough. You have to unlock the right to use it.

Inputs are D-pad only

GTA 5 console codes mixed face buttons, triggers, bumpers and the D-pad, so PlayStation and Xbox lists looked different. GTA 6 codes use only the four D-pad directions, which makes every sequence identical on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Only the buttons you hold change: L1 and R1 on PS5, LB and RB on Xbox.

The phone is still involved

GTA 5 also let you dial cheats as phone numbers on the in-game phone. GTA 6 keeps the phone as the gateway in a different form. You take the phone out with Up on the D-pad, hold the shoulder buttons, and enter the arrows.

More cheats, and new kinds

GTA 6 has 61 cheats across five groups. Alongside the familiar types, such as money, ammo, vehicle spawns, weather and wanted level, there is a large new group of 16 screen filters, plus physics cheats like Attack Force and Vehicle Force.

What stayed the same

Cheats are still a single-player feature, and they still switch off trophies and achievements while active. In GTA 6 they also disable saving for the session, so the old habit of keeping a separate save is more important than ever. See cheats, trophies and saving.

Do GTA 5 codes work in GTA 6?

No. The input method and the codes are entirely different. Old GTA 5 button sequences do nothing in GTA 6.

Cheat-by-cheat notes

$250,000

Adds $250,000 to your balance instantly. To unlock it, finish the main story. The input is 8 presses: Up, Right, Down, Left, Up, Left, Down, Right. Use it on a throwaway save to test-drive expensive purchases before committing real story money.

Unlimited Focus

Your Focus meter never drains. To unlock it, finish the main story. The input is 10 presses: Down, Up, Up, Down, Up, Down, Up, Down, Up, Right. Pairs well with the shooting-range medal grind if you want to practise aim without a meter running out.

Attack Force

Bullets and melee hits launch targets on impact. To unlock it, finish the main story. The input is 9 presses: Down, Up, Left, Left, Right, Down, Down, Up, Down. It shares its first 6 or more inputs with Exploding Balls, Vehicle Force, Exploding Bottles, so slow down at the end. Shares its first five inputs with Exploding Balls and Vehicle Force.

Cycle Weather

Cycles through weather conditions. To unlock it, finish the main story. A few guides say you must reveal the whole map instead, so sources differ. The input is 7 presses: Down, Right, Up, Down, Right, Up, Right. It shares its first 6 or more inputs with Move Time Forward, Pause Time of Day, so slow down at the end. Enter it repeatedly to step through conditions until you reach the one you want.

Frequently asked questions

Do GTA 5 cheat codes work in GTA 6?

No. GTA 6 uses a different input method and different codes.

Can you use cheats from the start in GTA 6?

No. Unlike GTA 5, cheats in GTA 6 unlock only after the main story, and most need an extra challenge.

Are GTA 6 cheats the same on PS5 and Xbox?

Yes. The D-pad sequences are identical. Only the shoulder buttons differ.
