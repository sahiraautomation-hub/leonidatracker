GTA 6 Strangers and Freaks: Every Side Character and Trophy

MissionsBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

Strangers and Freaks are the side characters you meet across Leonida, each with a short chain of optional missions. They matter for two reasons: a Silver trophy for finishing eight of the strands, and separate trophies for several individual characters. This list is spoiler-light and sticks to who, when and what you get.

Side missions usually appear on the map as optional missions. Some can only be played as Jason or as Lucia.

The Strangers & Freaks trophy

The Silver trophy asks for 8 stranger mission strands. Not every side character counts. Launch guides agree that Kirk, Kyle, Yago and Pilot Pete count toward it. Characters who have a trophy of their own, such as Billy, Andy, Tyrone, Bret and Randy, do not. The remaining strands that count are still being confirmed as players finish the game.

Character trophies

  • This Was No Boat Accident: finish Kenton's diving strand.
  • Baited My Tackle: beat Cal in a fishing challenge.
  • The Lone Rider: finish Billy's strand.
  • Fair Game: hunt a unique animal for Bret (Jason only).
  • Supply & Demand: finish Randy's Wish List strand.
  • Road to Rehabilitation: finish Andy's strand (Lucia only).
  • Spin On This!: finish Tyrone's strand (Jason only).

Chapter 1 strangers

Three characters around the Keys open early. Kenton offers treasure dives after the mission Island Dreams. He has seven sets of dive sites, and five of the seven are reported to count toward 100%. His dives are also good early money. Kirk sends you looking for the Skunk Ape, with some missions available only at night. Shirley, Jason's neighbour, has two short errands that can be missed for good when Chapter 1 ends.

Chapter 2 strangers

Kyle has three missions around a farm. Billy runs with a dirt bike crew at the Thrillbilly Mud Club, across five missions: Thrillbillies; Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab; Mall Rats; The Impaler; and Hometown Heroes. Bret Vogel is a hunting contact for Jason. Moses has ongoing car deliveries, which also unlock car registration and the Scooter Bros app.

Chapter 3 and later

Yago brings street racing. One of his races is time-limited, so do it when it appears. Andy has a Lucia-only strand, and Tyrone a Jason-only one that opens after a story mission. Randy opens his Wish List midway through the chapter. Pilot Pete offers flying jobs for Jason.

Frequently asked questions

How many Strangers and Freaks do you need for the trophy?

Eight mission strands. Kirk, Kyle, Yago and Pilot Pete are confirmed to count.

Are Billy's missions part of the main story?

No. Thrillbillies and the rest of Billy's missions are optional, although they first appear during Chapter 2.

Can you miss any side missions in GTA 6?

Yes. Shirley's Chapter 1 errands disappear when the chapter ends, and one of Yago's races in Chapter 3 is time-limited.
