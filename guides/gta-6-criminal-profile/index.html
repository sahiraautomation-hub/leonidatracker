GTA 6 Criminal Profile: All 5 Levels and How to Change Yours

BasicsBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

Quick answer: GTA 6's criminal record is called the Criminal Profile. It has five levels: Professional, Hardened, Violent, Psycho and Irredeemable. Killing civilians and police pushes it up, while stealth and non-lethal play bring it down. Irredeemable is permanent for that character, and the profile changes how the police treat you.

The five levels

  • Professional: clean jobs, little or no bloodshed.
  • Hardened
  • Violent
  • Psycho: frequent killing of civilians and police.
  • Irredeemable: the end of the scale, reached by staying at Psycho behaviour for too long.

Jason and Lucia each have their own profile, so the two characters can end up with very different reputations.

What moves your profile

Actions that shift the Criminal Profile
Toward ProfessionalToward Psycho
Stealth and undetected crimesKilling civilians
Non-lethal takedowns and limb shotsKilling police officers
Disabling alarms, destroying footage and leaving cleanlySustained, needless violence

What your profile changes

The main effect is on the police. At Psycho and Irredeemable, officers are more likely to open fire on sight, and at Psycho they are reported to stop accepting a surrender. Your profile also decides which actions build your maximum Focus, the slow-motion ability. A Professional gains Focus from stealthy and peaceful play, while a Psycho gains it from headshots. An Irredeemable character is reported to get no Focus bonus at all.

Irredeemable is permanent

The first four levels move up and down as your behaviour changes. Irredeemable does not. Once a character reaches it, guides agree it cannot be undone for that character. Make a manual save in a separate slot before any rampage. See how to save.

How to get back to Professional

A single bad moment does not wreck a clean profile, and recovery is possible from Hardened, Violent and Psycho. Play quietly for a while: use takedowns instead of gunfire, threaten instead of shoot during store robberies, switch off alarms, wipe the footage and leave without a chase.

The Actions Speak Louder trophy

A hidden Bronze trophy, Actions Speak Louder, is awarded for performing 500 actions that change your Criminal Profile. It counts changes in either direction, so normal play earns it over time.

Frequently asked questions

What are the Criminal Profile levels in GTA 6?

Professional, Hardened, Violent, Psycho and Irredeemable.

Can you undo Irredeemable in GTA 6?

No. Irredeemable is reported to be permanent for that character, so save before risking it.

Do Jason and Lucia share a Criminal Profile?

No. Each character has a separate profile.
