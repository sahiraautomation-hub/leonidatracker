GTA 6 Beginner Tips: 15 Things to Know in Your First Hours

BasicsBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

GTA 6 explains very little about its new systems, and a few of them can cost you hours if you learn them the hard way. These 15 tips cover the mistakes new players make most often. There are no story spoilers.

Protect your progress

  • 1. Save by hand. Autosave only runs when a mission ends, and there is no mission replay. Use Pause, Game, Save Game. See how to save.
  • 2. Finish the Prologue in one sitting. Manual saving is reported to be unavailable until it ends.
  • 3. Keep one save per chapter. If you miss something, you will have a way back.

Protect your money

  • 4. Never carry your savings. Cash in your pocket is lost when you die or get arrested.
  • 5. Use the floorboard stash. Before you have a bank account, stash cash under the loose floorboards in Jason's bedroom.
  • 6. Bank it after Buck Me. That Chapter 1 mission opens your BuckMe account. More in keeping money safe.

Weapons and gear

  • 7. Buy your guns. Bought guns can be recovered after you die. Guns taken from enemies cannot. Details in the gun shop guide.
  • 8. Use your trunk. Store found weapons and spare outfits in your personal vehicle.
  • 9. Holster in public. Tap L1 or LB. People call the police when they see a drawn gun.

Police and stealth

  • 10. Read the Heat icons. The red icons under your wanted stars show what police know: outfit, face, car, weapon or partner. Change those things to escape. See the wanted level guide.
  • 11. Use Focus. Click both sticks, L3 and R3. In a fight it slows time. Outside combat it highlights enemies, loot and usable objects.
  • 12. Scan cars before stealing them. Hold Up on the D-pad for WAiNK. It shows alarms and trackers.

Getting around and getting stronger

  • 13. Take a cab to travel fast. Call a taxi from your phone and pay extra to skip the ride. See fast travel.
  • 14. Train a little each day. Strength raises your health and Cardio your stamina, but both fade if you stop. See the stats guide.
  • 15. Mind your Criminal Profile. Needless killing pushes it toward a permanent worst level. See Criminal Profile.

One thing to do before Chapter 1 ends

Some content only exists in Chapter 1. Before you start the mission Tourist Trap, check the missable content list so nothing is locked out.

Frequently asked questions

What should I do first in GTA 6?

Finish the Prologue, make a manual save, stash your cash under Jason's floorboards and buy a gun during the Gear Up mission.

Does GTA 6 have mission replay?

No. Keep manual saves if you want to go back.

How do you use Focus in GTA 6?

Click both sticks together: L3 and R3 on PS5, or both stick clicks on Xbox.
