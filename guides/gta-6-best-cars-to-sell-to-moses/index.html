Best Cars to Sell to Moses in GTA 6: What Guides Report He Pays

MoneyBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·5 min read

Quick answer: the best-supported big payouts from Moses are for the vehicles he asks you to steal. Two guides name the Nightblade as his top payout, reported at roughly $6,300 to $7,150. The Bandito is the next one that two guides agree on, at around $3,400. For ordinary street cars, reported prices run from $150 to just over $5,000. Every figure on this page is a reported one, and each changes with the condition of the car.

How Moses prices a car

Moses works out of Watkins Auto Parts in Port Gellhorn and starts buying cars in Chapter 2, after the story mission named after him. The car selling guide covers him alongside the other buyers.

His price depends on the model and on the state of that particular car. Guides agree that damage lowers the payout and that a car in perfect condition earns its full value. The same model is not always worth the same amount: two guides report a Willard Grouse at $150 one time and $275 another.

You do not have to guess. Scan a car with the WAiNK app before you steal it and the card shows its sell value, its Cost to Register and whether it has a tracker.

The best payouts: cars Moses asks for

Moses sometimes asks for a particular car and tells you where to look. Three guides describe these requests, and the cars involved are reported to pay more than almost anything you find on your own. The table lists the request cars that at least two guides name.

One guide reports an extra $500 on most of these jobs for bringing the car in good condition. Single guides also list a Vamos at about $4,200, a Karin Futo at $1,650 and an Annis Hellion at $1,540 as request cars. No second guide confirms those three yet.

Street cars: what is worth the drive

Outside those requests, reported values swing widely, and fewer guides have checked each car. Treat this table as a rough guide and trust the WAiNK scan in front of you over any list.

The pattern in the reports is simple. Working vehicles such as the Caracara 4x4 and the Speedo van are reported well above $2,000, while a tired Willard Grouse is reported at a few hundred dollars at best. One guide's advice on the Grouse is to skip it. If a scan shows only a few hundred dollars, the trip to Port Gellhorn is rarely worth your time.

Cars Moses will not buy

Three guides agree on two firm rules. Moses refuses any car with a tracker, and he refuses cars stolen close to his own garage. One guide adds that he turns away vehicles towing a trailer.

The tracker rule is why the most valuable cars cannot go to him. Guides report that the expensive sports cars carry trackers, so Moses will not take them. From Chapter 3 you can take cars with a Standard Tracker to a Pay 'n' Spray, and later the high-end cars on Randy's Wish List are reported to pay far more than anything Moses buys.

Limits: the wait and the daily budget

You cannot sell to Moses without a break, but guides do not agree on how the limit works. One reports a wait of about two minutes of real time after each sale, and notes that another guide says a few in-game hours. A different guide says only that Moses will not take a second car while he is still dealing with the last one. One guide reports that sleeping at a safehouse skips the wait.

One guide also reports a daily budget. Once Moses has spent it he stops buying until the next day, and a single car worth around $5,000 was enough to reach it early on. The same guide says the budget grows over time. No guide gives an exact figure, so none is given here.

Sell it or keep it?

Moses also registers cars, which makes them yours for good. Three guides agree that your first registration is free. After that you pay the Cost to Register shown on the WAiNK card, and reported costs are several times the sell value. One guide gives the example of a car that sells for $750 and costs $4,750 to register.

So sell the ordinary cars and keep only the ones you will really drive. The guide to keeping and registering cars explains the steps, and making money by chapter shows where Moses fits into your Chapter 2 income.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best car to sell to Moses in GTA 6?

Two guides name the Nightblade, which Moses asks for himself. It is reported to pay roughly $6,300 to $7,150. The Bandito is the next one two guides agree on, at around $3,400.

How much does Moses pay for cars?

Reported prices run from $150 for a worn Willard Grouse to just over $5,000 for a Caracara 4x4. The cars he asks for by name can pay more. Condition changes every price.

Why has Moses stopped buying my cars?

He needs time between sales, and one guide reports a daily budget that stops him buying once it is spent. He also refuses cars with a tracker and cars stolen near his garage.

Does damage lower the price Moses pays?

Yes. Guides agree that damage lowers the payout and that a car in perfect condition earns its full value, so drive carefully on the way.
