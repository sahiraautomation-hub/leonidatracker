GTA 6 Race Venues: Gellhorn Raceway, Thrillbilly Mud Club and Boone Speedway

WorldBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

Three race venues in GTA 6 share one big reward. Ten first-place finishes across them unlock the Repair Vehicle cheat.

The venues

  • Gellhorn Raceway
  • Thrillbilly Mud Club
  • Boone Speedway

Tick each venue in the Tracker once you have raced there, and log total wins on the race-wins counter.

Planning ten wins

Pick the venue that suits your driving and stick with it until the event feels routine. A mud club rewards different handling to an oval speedway, so a vehicle that wins at one may struggle at the other.

The payoff

Repair Vehicle fully fixes your current ride. Its code is Right, Down, Down, Right, Down, Right, Right, Down. It was added to the cheats database on 28 November 2026.

Cheat-by-cheat notes

Repair Vehicle

Fully repairs your current vehicle. To unlock it, take first place in 10 races at Gellhorn Raceway, Thrillbilly Mud Club and Boone Speedway. The input is 8 presses: Right, Down, Down, Right, Down, Right, Right, Down. It shares its first 7 or more inputs with Unlimited Nitrous and Fuel, so slow down at the end. Track your wins per venue in the Tracker so you know where you still need podiums.

Unlimited Nitrous and Fuel

Nitrous and fuel never run out. To unlock it, complete 50 stunt jumps. The input is 8 presses: Right, Down, Down, Right, Down, Right, Right, Left. It shares its first 7 or more inputs with Repair Vehicle, so slow down at the end. Log each completed jump in the Tracker as you go. It is much easier than recounting later.
