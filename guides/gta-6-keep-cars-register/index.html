How to Keep Stolen Cars in GTA 6: Registration, Scooter Bros and the Impound

VehiclesBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

Quick answer: you keep a stolen car by registering it, which swaps its plates and makes it your personal vehicle. Moses registers cars with no tracker from Chapter 2, and any Pay 'n' Spray registers cars from Chapter 3. You cannot keep stolen cars in Chapter 1.

What you can keep, by chapter

  • Chapter 1: you cannot keep stolen cars. You have Jason's own car, and you can call Joey from your contacts to bring it back.
  • Chapter 2: the story mission Moses unlocks registration at his garage in Port Gellhorn. He registers cars that have no tracker.
  • Chapter 3: any Pay 'n' Spray can register cars, including those with a Standard Tracker.

How to register a car

  1. Scan the car with the WAiNK app before stealing it. The scan shows its tracker status, sell value and Cost to Register.
  2. Steal it and lose any wanted level.
  3. Drive to Moses or a Pay 'n' Spray.
  4. Choose Register. The plates are swapped and the car becomes yours.
  5. Park it in a garage or parking space you own.

The Cost to Register is several times the sell value on many cars, so decide whether the car is really worth keeping before you pay.

Scooter Bros: call your cars

Finishing the Moses mission also installs the Scooter Bros app on your phone. It lists your registered vehicles and delivers one to you for a fee. It is also how you recover a personal vehicle that has been lost or destroyed.

If your car is impounded

When the police impound a personal vehicle you have two options. Pay Scooter Bros to fetch it, or go to the impound lot and drive it out yourself.

More cars means more garages

Each registered car needs somewhere to live. Garages bought through the Vice City Real Estate app each add a space. See all eight garages and prices.

The On the Spot trophy

Registering 10 vehicles to the Scooter Bros app earns On the Spot, a Bronze trophy. If you plan to buy every garage anyway, this comes naturally as you fill them.

Frequently asked questions

How do you save a car in GTA 6?

Register it at Moses' garage (Chapter 2) or a Pay 'n' Spray (Chapter 3). Unregistered stolen cars are lost when you leave them, die or get arrested.

How do I call my car in GTA 6?

Use the Scooter Bros app on your phone, which unlocks with the Moses mission in Chapter 2. In Chapter 1, call Joey for Jason's car.

How do I get my car back from the impound?

Pay Scooter Bros to recover it, or drive it out of the impound lot yourself.
