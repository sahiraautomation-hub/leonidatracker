GTA 6 Wanted Level Cheats: Raise Wanted Level and Raise Heat Level

CheatsBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

GTA 6 has two cheats that turn up the pressure: Raise Heat Level and Raise Wanted Level. Both come from the same challenge, losing a three-star wanted level, and their codes differ by a single press.

The codes side by side

Raise Heat Level is Left, Down, Right, Down, Up, Down, Up. Raise Wanted Level is Left, Down, Right, Down, Up, Right, Up. Only the sixth input changes, Down for heat and Right for wanted, so slow down at that point.

One challenge, two rewards

Losing a three-star wanted level 25 times unlocks Raise Heat Level, and reaching 50 unlocks Raise Wanted Level. Track the running total in the Tracker so you know when the first reward lands.

Is there a lower wanted level cheat?

No lower-wanted cheat has been found in the 61-cheat list. To shed stars you still need to break line of sight, change vehicles or use a Pay 'n' Spray, which unlocks early in Chapter 1.

Losing a wanted level the normal way

Both cheats come from escaping three-star wanted levels, so it pays to get good at it. The wanted level and Heat guide explains what each star colour means and how to clear every Heat icon.

Cheat-by-cheat notes

Raise Heat Level

Raises your heat level across the map. To unlock it, lose a three-star wanted level 25 times. The input is 7 presses: Left, Down, Right, Down, Up, Down, Up. It shares its first 5 or more inputs with Raise Wanted Level, so slow down at the end. The same counter also unlocks Raise Wanted Level at 50. Only the sixth input differs from Raise Wanted Level (Down here, Right there). Double-check before you enter it.

Raise Wanted Level

Raises your wanted level. To unlock it, lose a three-star wanted level 50 times. The input is 7 presses: Left, Down, Right, Down, Up, Right, Up. It shares its first 5 or more inputs with Raise Heat Level, so slow down at the end. The same counter also unlocks Raise Heat Level at 25. You unlock Raise Heat Level halfway through this challenge, so both come from the same grind.
