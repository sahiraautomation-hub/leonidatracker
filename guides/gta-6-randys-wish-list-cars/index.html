GTA 6 Randy's Wish List Cars: Every Car and Where to Find It

VehiclesBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·6 min read

Quick answer: guides do not agree on how many cars are on Randy's Wish List. Fifteen cars appear on every detailed list, and up to five more appear on some. Most of them turn up at the big houses in Capri. A cluster sits in mansion garages on Starfish Island, three are at the car dealerships in Southside, and a few have one-off spots such as a junkyard in Hamlet. Spawns are not fixed, so the table below only lists places that at least two guides agree on, and says so where they do not.

The cars and where guides agree

Sources differ on the total. Two car lists name all 20 vehicles below. Two other detailed guides are shorter: one lists 16 cars, and one says the list starts with 15. The Coquette D10, the Cypher, the Carbonizzare and the Neon are on the two longer lists only. One guide says the Coquette D10 comes from a separate job for Randy, and that the Zorrusso is the car from the story job that opens the list, not an entry on it. The other 15 cars are on every list. Check the list in your own game before you hunt for those five.

Locations are harder. Guides often put the same car in different places, because most of these cars can appear in more than one spot. A place is listed here only when at least two guides name it. Where they do not agree, the table says so.

Where to look first

Capri is the place to start. More than half the list is reported at the large houses there, parked on driveways, behind gates or inside garages. A slow lap of the neighbourhood, scanning as you go, can cover the Paragon, the '96 Infernus, the Toros, the Neon, the Growler and the Carbonizzare.

Starfish Island is the second stop. Two guides put the Vitello, the Windsor Drop and the Baller ST-D in mansion garages there, and one says all three share a single property. The Furia is reported at another mansion on the island. One guide mentions a guard and locked garage doors.

Southside has the dealerships. The Banshee is reported at the Sunrise dealership, the Cypher at the Übermacht dealership and the Growler at a used car dealership in the same district.

That leaves the one-off spots: the Gauntlet Hellfire at a junkyard in Hamlet, the Jugular outside the Bite shop north of Ocean Beach, the Coquette D10 on Waxman Island and the Itali RSX in a house garage on the Vespucci Islands.

If a car is not where a guide says

  • Scan parked cars with the WAiNK app before you commit. It identifies the car and shows whether it has a tracker.
  • Come back at a different time of day. Two guides tie some cars to a time of day, or say they are not there on every visit.
  • Ask Randy. Guides report that he gives leads on cars he still needs, and that calling him can produce a job for one of them.
  • One guide reports that saving and reloading refreshes which cars are parked in an area. That comes from a single source, so treat it as a tip to try.

This will happen. Only a handful of these cars are described as fixed spawns, and guides disagree even on those. Treat every location as a place to check, not a promise.

Stealing and delivering them

Many of these cars cannot be opened without the Key Cloner, which you get from Randy's own job in Chapter 3. The Randy's Wish List guide covers how the list unlocks and what the tool does.

Deliveries go to Randy at Leonida Auto World, his garage in western Vice City. He pays more for a car with no damage. One guide puts the bonus for a perfect car at double, and launch guides report $30,000 or more per car. Drive gently and lose any wanted level first. One guide suggests a stop at a repair shop if you have scraped the car.

If you would rather keep one of these cars, the guide to keeping cars explains registration, and the garages guide lists where to store it. For ordinary cars, the car selling guide compares the other buyers.

What finishing the list gives you

Two guides agree that delivering every car unlocks a way to handle cars with advanced trackers. One says it lets you sell them. The other names the reward as the Advanced Tracker Demobiliser, a tool for removing trackers.

Two guides also describe an extra classic, a Mamba GT at Leaf Links. Randy is reported to reveal it only after you have handed in a good share of the list. One guide puts that at about ten cars.

Randy's strand also has its own trophy, Supply & Demand, and is reported to count toward 100%. The trophy list has the details, and the 100% checklist shows what else counts.

Frequently asked questions

How many cars are on Randy's Wish List in GTA 6?

Sources differ. Fifteen cars appear on every detailed list, one guide lists 16 and two list 20. Check Randy's list in your own game.

Where are most of Randy's cars in GTA 6?

Capri. More than half the list is reported at the large houses there. Starfish Island and the Southside dealerships cover most of the rest.

Where do you deliver cars to Randy?

To Randy at Leonida Auto World, his garage in western Vice City. He pays more for a car with no damage.

Why is a car not at the location in a guide?

Most of these cars can appear in more than one place and are not there on every visit. Check again at another time of day, or ask Randy for a lead.
