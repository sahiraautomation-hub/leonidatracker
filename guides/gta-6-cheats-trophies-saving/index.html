Do GTA 6 Cheats Disable Trophies, Achievements and Saving?

CheatsBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

Yes. Entering any cheat in GTA 6 disables saving, trophies and achievements for that session. The fix is simple: keep a clean save that cheats never touch.

What gets switched off

Once a cheat is active you cannot save the game, and trophies on PS5 or achievements on Xbox will not pop. The restriction covers the whole session, not just the moment the cheat is active. Reloading a save from before you used the cheat restores normal behaviour.

A two-slot routine

  • Slot A is your real playthrough. Never enter a cheat while it is loaded.
  • Before experimenting, make a manual save in Slot B.
  • Load Slot B, use cheats freely, and quit without saving.
  • Reload Slot A to continue earning trophies.

This takes seconds and removes the risk of losing hours of progress by accident.

Trophies you might want first

Some challenges overlap with trophies. Completing three collectible card sets earns the silver Don't Touch My Deck trophy, and 100% completion is tied to the A Long Road achievement. Finish those on your clean save before you lean on cheats. The 100% completion guide lists what counts.

Cheats worth trying on a backup save

Once you have a backup slot, a few cheats are especially useful for testing. The $250,000 cheat lets you preview purchases before spending real money. Cycle Weather and, once unlocked, Pause Time of Day are ideal for screenshots. Attack Force and Vehicle Force are simply fun. None of these affect your clean save, as long as you quit without saving.

How saving works in general

GTA 6 only autosaves when a mission ends, and it has no mission replay. The saving guide explains manual saves and a slot routine that protects both trophies and missable content.

Frequently asked questions

Does turning a cheat off restore trophies?

Reload a save from before you used the cheat to get trophies and saving back.

Do cheats affect my 100% completion?

They block saving in that session, so any progress made while cheating is lost when you reload. Do completion work on a clean save.

Do I lose unlocked cheats if I reload?

Cheat unlocks come from challenges you completed on your save, so they stay as long as that save had them.
