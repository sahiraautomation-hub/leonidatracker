GTA 6 Magazines, Books and Comics: 34 Reads and 100% Requirement

CollectiblesBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

Reading material is its own collectible in GTA 6. There are 27 magazines, books and comics to find, plus seven book swaps, for 34 in total.

What counts

  • 27 magazines, books and comics.
  • 7 book swaps.
  • 30 of the 34 count toward 100% completion.

Keeping them separate

Book swaps are listed apart from the 27 reads, so track them on their own counters. That way you can see at a glance whether you are short on reads or on swaps when you get close to 30.

Pairing with other runs

Log each read in the Tracker as you pick it up, so your total is right when you get close to 30.

Choosing which four to skip

Thirty of the 34 count toward 100%, so you can leave four. Do not decide in advance. Collect normally, and when you reach 30 leave the remaining four, which are usually the ones that would take a special trip. If you are chasing every entry for its own sake, 34 is the full set.
