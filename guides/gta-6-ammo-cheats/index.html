GTA 6 Ammo Cheats: Max Ammo, Infinite Ammo and No Reload

CheatsBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

The three ammo cheats in GTA 6 all come from shooting ranges. Each needs 25 medals at a different tier, and all three codes share the same first seven inputs.

Medal requirements

  • Max Ammo: 25 medals at bronze or better.
  • Infinite Ammo: 25 medals at silver or better.
  • No Reload: 25 gold medals.

Because a better medal also counts for the lower tiers, every gold you earn pushes all three counters at once. Aim for gold from the start and you may unlock all three together.

Remembering the codes

All three start Down, Up, Right, Down, Left, Left, Up. Max Ammo ends Up, Down, Left. Infinite Ammo ends Down, Down, Down. No Reload ends Down, Down, Up. A handy way to keep them apart: Infinite Ammo is the one that just keeps going down.

Practising for gold

Shooting-range runs reward steady aim more than speed. Run the same range several times in a row so the target pattern becomes familiar, then move on. Log your medal counts in the Tracker after each session so you know which tier you are short on.

Cheat-by-cheat notes

Max Ammo

Fills all your ammo to the maximum. To unlock it, earn 25 bronze-or-better medals at shooting ranges. The input is 10 presses: Down, Up, Right, Down, Left, Left, Up, Up, Down, Left. It shares its first 7 or more inputs with Infinite Ammo, No Reload, so slow down at the end. The three ammo cheats share their first seven inputs. Only the last three change.

Infinite Ammo

Your ammo never runs out. To unlock it, earn 25 silver-or-better medals at shooting ranges. The input is 10 presses: Down, Up, Right, Down, Left, Left, Up, Down, Down, Down. It shares its first 7 or more inputs with Max Ammo, No Reload, so slow down at the end. Ends Down, Down, Down. Think of it as the bottom-heavy ammo code.

No Reload

Infinite ammo with no reloading. To unlock it, earn 25 gold medals at shooting ranges. The input is 10 presses: Down, Up, Right, Down, Left, Left, Up, Down, Down, Up. It shares its first 7 or more inputs with Max Ammo, Infinite Ammo, so slow down at the end. Same as Infinite Ammo until the final press, which is Up instead of Down.

Frequently asked questions

Do gold medals count toward Max Ammo?

Yes. Max Ammo accepts bronze or better, and Infinite Ammo accepts silver or better, so gold counts for both.

What is the difference between Infinite Ammo and No Reload?

Infinite Ammo means you never run out. No Reload also removes the need to reload.

Are ammo cheats the same on Xbox?

Yes. Enter the same D-pad sequence while holding LB and pressing RB.
