GTA 6 Chapter 2 Missions: Paradise Lost (All 11 in Order)

MissionsBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

Chapter 2, Paradise Lost, has 11 main story missions and is centred on Port Gellhorn. It is the shortest of the first four chapters, but it unlocks several systems you will use for the rest of the game.

What Chapter 2 unlocks

  • Moses unlocks car registration and selling at Moses' garage in Port Gellhorn, plus the Scooter Bros app for calling your cars.
  • Daily Driver leads to the Vice City Real Estate app, where you can buy garages.
  • Breakfast Run unlocks switching between Jason and Lucia. Hold Right on the D-pad and press R1 on PS5 or RB on Xbox.

Two missions that show up during this chapter, Thrillbillies and Twist Wrist, Smash, Grab, are optional. They belong to Billy's side strand at the Thrillbilly Mud Club, which continues with Mall Rats, The Impaler and Hometown Heroes.

Before you start

If you want to keep Chapter 1 side content tidy, now is a good moment to log your collectible counts in the Tracker. Chapter 2 opens up more of the map, and it is easy to lose track once you spread out.

Using this checklist

Mission names are blurred in the Tracker until you tap them, so you can tick progress without reading ahead. On this page they are shown in full.

Where Chapter 2 leaves you

Finishing Paradise Lost brings you to 33 of the 91 story missions, a little over a third of the way through the main story. If you track side content as you go, this is a natural point to check your counters against the Progress menu and correct anything you missed.

Keep a clean save before moving on

Make a manual save in a separate slot at the end of Chapter 2. It gives you a safe point to return to if you later want to replay a section, and it is good practice for the cheats phase after the credits, when you will want a save that cheats never touch. More in cheats, trophies and saving.
