GTA 6 Challenges Explained: All 8 Tracks and Their Rewards

ProgressBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·3 min read

Challenges in GTA 6 are grouped into eight tracks in the Progress menu. Each track unlocks two vehicle spawn cheats: one at five completed challenges and one when the track is finished. Here is what every track gives you.

Track rewards at a glance

  • Survival: Airboat 09 at five, Hauners Rhino Tank for the full track.
  • Combat: Speeder 700 GT at five, Bravado Banshee for the full track.
  • Fitness: Tuff BMX at five, '65 Vapid Dominator for the full track.
  • Shopping: Maibatsu Boomazoom at five, Truffade Thrax for the full track.
  • Exploration: Nagasaki Sea Sparrow at five, Nagasaki Carbon RS for the full track.
  • Vehicle: Lapwing Mallard at five, Progen Emerus for the full track.
  • Sports: Vapid Stanier G2 Taxi at five, Brimstone Golfer for the full track.
  • Criminal: Maibatsu Sanchez at five, Western Zombie Bobber for the full track.

The capstone reward

Finishing every challenge on every track unlocks the Hauners P-996 Lazer, the last vehicle cheat most players will earn. It is the single hardest cheat to unlock in the game.

Five first, then finish

The quickest way to build a garage of spawnable vehicles is to take all eight tracks to five challenges before finishing any of them. That gives eight vehicles for 40 challenges. After that, pick the track whose full reward you want most. The Rhino Tank (survival) and the Banshee (combat) are obvious candidates.

Where to find them

Pause, open the Map, switch to the Progress tab and choose the Challenges card. Remember that vehicle cheats, like all cheats, only become usable after the main story. Count each track in the Tracker and the Cheat Unlock Planner will show which vehicle is next.
