How to Make Money Fast in GTA 6: The Best Method in Every Chapter

MoneyBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·3 min read

Quick answer: robberies and car sales. In Chapter 1, do Kenton's treasure dives and do not grind. In Chapter 2, sell untracked cars to Moses for a reported $1,000 or more each. Chapter 3 adds jewelry stores and Randy's Wish List, and bank vaults near the end of Chapter 4 pay the most. Figures below are payouts reported by players in launch week.

The short answer

Robberies are the fastest money in GTA 6 for most of the game. Early on, that means small stores and criminal stashes. In Chapter 2 you can sell stolen cars. Chapter 3 adds jewelry stores and high-end car deliveries, and the biggest single payouts come from bank vaults near the end of Chapter 4.

Chapter 1: earn a little, protect it

Do not grind in Chapter 1. Launch guides report that cash from Chapter 1 does not follow you into Chapter 2, so large savings here are wasted. Earn what you need for a couple of guns and move on.

The safest early income is Kenton's treasure dives, which open after the mission Island Dreams. Kenton pays cash on the spot, and nobody shoots at you underwater. A first wreck is reported to pay about $1,400. Store registers add small amounts, and loot such as drugs, jewelry and marked bills can be dropped at Brian's lockup, which opens after Just a Scratch.

Chapter 2: sell cars to Moses

The mission Moses opens his garage in Port Gellhorn. He pays $1,000 or more for a stolen car, as long as it has no tracker. This is the first reliable, repeatable income in the game. Scan a car with the WAiNK app before you take it to see whether it is tracked and what it sells for. Full details are in the car selling guide.

Chapter 3: jewelry stores and supercars

Three things unlock in Chapter 3. Pay 'n' Spray shops start buying cars that have a Standard Tracker, for around $4,000 each. Jewelry stores open up as robbery targets and are reported to pay $10,000 to $25,000 per store. See the jewelry store guide. Midway through the chapter, Randy's Wish List pays $30,000 or more for each high-end car delivered in perfect condition.

Chapter 4: bank vaults

Near the end of Chapter 4, an optional mission opens up bank vault robberies. A vault is reported to pay up to $205,000, the largest repeatable payout in the game. It also brings a heavy police response, so plan the getaway first. The bank vault guide covers the method.

Keep what you earn

Cash you are carrying is lost when you die or get arrested. Bank it as soon as you can. Until the Buck Me mission opens your bank account, use the stash under the floorboards in Jason's bedroom. The money safety guide explains both.

After the story

Once the credits roll, the $250,000 cheat becomes available. It disables saving and trophies for the session, so it is for testing purchases, not for building a real balance.

Frequently asked questions

What is the fastest way to make money in GTA 6?

Bank vault robberies, which are reported to pay up to $205,000 each, but they only unlock near the end of Chapter 4. Before that, jewelry stores and car sales are the best earners.

How do I make money early in GTA 6?

Do Kenton's treasure dives after Island Dreams, rob small stores, and sell loot at Brian's lockup. From Chapter 2, sell stolen cars to Moses.

Is there a money cheat in GTA 6?

Yes. A $250,000 cheat unlocks after the main story. It disables saving and trophies while active.
