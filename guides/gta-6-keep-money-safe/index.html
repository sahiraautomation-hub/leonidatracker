How to Keep Your Money Safe in GTA 6: The Stash and BuckMe

MoneyBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

Quick answer: yes, you lose the cash you are carrying when you die or get busted. Money in Jason's floorboard stash or your BuckMe bank account is safe. Use the stash until the Chapter 1 mission Buck Me, then deposit at any ATM. Empty the stash before Tourist Trap.

What you lose, and when

Money you are carrying disappears if you are Wasted or Busted. Money that has been stashed or banked is safe. That makes the rule simple: after any job, put the cash away before you do anything risky.

Before you have a bank: the floorboard stash

In the opening hours you have no bank account. Go into Jason's bedroom and interact with the loose floorboards beside the bed. You can deposit cash there and take it out later. It is basic, but it is the only protection you have until the bank opens.

Players also report that some mission pay and money from contacts is delivered to the mailbox outside Jason's house, so check it when you come home.

BuckMe: your bank account

The Chapter 1 mission Buck Me takes Jason and Lucia to the bank and opens a BuckMe account. From then on, deposit cash at an ATM and it is safe. Banked money can also be used to pay in shops, so there is little reason to carry more than pocket change.

Empty the stash before Chapter 2

Launch guides report that cash left in the floorboard stash does not follow you into Chapter 2. Before you start Tourist Trap, the point of no return for Chapter 1, empty the stash and bank it or spend it. That mission is listed in the Chapter 1 mission guide.

A safe routine

  1. Finish a robbery or sale.
  2. Lose any wanted level first. Dying on the way to the bank costs you the lot.
  3. Deposit at the nearest ATM, or at the stash early on.
  4. Make a manual save. See how to save.

Frequently asked questions

Do you lose money when you die in GTA 6?

Yes. Cash you are carrying is lost when you die or get arrested. Stashed and banked money is safe.

How do I get a bank account in GTA 6?

Complete the Chapter 1 mission Buck Me. It opens your BuckMe account, and you can then deposit at ATMs.

Where is the money stash in GTA 6?

Under the loose floorboards beside the bed in Jason's bedroom.
