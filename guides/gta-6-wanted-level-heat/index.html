GTA 6 Wanted Level and Heat System: How to Lose the Police

BasicsBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·3 min read

Losing the police in GTA 6 is no longer just a matter of driving away. The wanted level now goes up to six stars, and a second layer called Heat records what officers actually know about you. Clear what they know and they cannot pick you out again.

Two layers: stars and Heat

Stars show how hard the police are responding, from one to six. Heat is the set of red icons under the stars. Each icon is a detail the police have on you, taken from witnesses, cameras or their own eyes. The more icons you carry, the easier you are to find again.

The Heat icons and how to clear each one

  • Outfit: change clothes. Keep a spare outfit in your vehicle's trunk.
  • Appearance: cover your face with a mask or a helmet.
  • Vehicle: switch cars, or respray at a Pay 'n' Spray.
  • Weapon: holster it or put it in the trunk so nobody can see it.
  • Partner: split up. Hold Right on the D-pad to open the partner menu and send them away.

Clearing an icon takes effect straight away, so a quick change of clothes and car after a chase is often all you need.

How to lose a wanted level, step by step

  1. Break line of sight. Alleys, car parks and tight corners work better than open roads.
  2. Get out of the search area marked on the map while the stars are grey. They flash and then clear.
  3. Deal with every red Heat icon: clothes, face, vehicle, weapon, partner.
  4. Stay away from the area until the Heat fades. Being spotted there, or committing another crime, brings the wanted level straight back.

Avoiding a wanted level in the first place

Most wanted levels start with a witness. Keep your weapon holstered in public, deal with cameras during robberies, and wear a mask when you plan a job. A tracked car can also bring attention, so scan vehicles with WAiNK before stealing them.

Wanted levels, trophies and cheats

  • Hot Together (Bronze): lose a four-star wanted level with your partner.
  • Losing a three-star wanted level 25 times unlocks the Raise Heat Level cheat, and 50 times unlocks Raise Wanted Level. See wanted level cheats.
  • Surviving six minutes at six stars unlocks the Migraine filter cheat. Count your escapes in the Tracker.

Frequently asked questions

How many wanted stars are in GTA 6?

Six.

How do you lose the police in GTA 6?

Break line of sight, leave the search area, then clear your Heat icons by changing clothes, covering your face, switching vehicle, holstering your weapon and splitting from your partner.

What do red wanted stars mean in GTA 6?

You have escaped the chase, but police still have Heat on you around your last known location. If you are spotted there, the chase restarts.
