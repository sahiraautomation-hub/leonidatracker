GTA 6 Stunt Jumps: 50 for Unlimited Nitrous and Fuel

CollectiblesBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

Stunt jumps are back in GTA 6, and they come with a reward: 50 completed jumps unlock the Unlimited Nitrous and Fuel cheat.

What you get

Unlimited Nitrous and Fuel means neither runs out on your vehicles. It is a player cheat, and its code has not been found yet.

Tracking jumps

Log each completed jump on the stunt-jumps counter in the Tracker. Recounting from memory after a long session is where most players lose track.

Pairing driving goals

Stunt-jump runs pair naturally with vehicle challenges, which unlock the Lapwing Mallard at five and the Progen Emerus when the track is finished.

Landing more jumps first time

Stunt jumps reward a straight, committed approach. Line up with the ramp from further back than feels necessary, hold your speed steady rather than braking at the lip, and pick a vehicle with good top speed and stable handling. A motorbike or a light sports car is usually easier to control in the air than a heavy SUV.

Fifty is the cheat target

The 50 on the Tracker counter is the number needed for Unlimited Nitrous and Fuel. The total number of stunt jumps in Leonida has not been confirmed here yet, so treat 50 as a milestone rather than the full count.

Race wins at the three venues
