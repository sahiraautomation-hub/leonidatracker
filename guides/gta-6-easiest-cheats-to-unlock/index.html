The Easiest GTA 6 Cheats to Unlock First

CheatsBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·6 min read

GTA 6 makes you earn its 61 cheats, but they are far from equally hard. Some arrive the moment the credits roll, some need one discovery, and some need a counter of just 20 or 25. Here is where to start if you want cheats quickly.

Tier 1: free with the story

Four cheats need nothing but the end credits: $250,000, Unlimited Focus, Attack Force and Cycle Weather. All four have confirmed codes, so you can use them the moment the story ends. If you only ever use four cheats, these are the ones. One note on Cycle Weather: most guides list it as a story unlock, but a few say you must reveal the whole map, so sources differ.

Tier 2: one discovery

Five cheats unlock from a single discovery rather than a grind. The MTL Pumper, HVY Bulldozer and Interstate Ambulance unlock once you discover the vehicle and it appears in the Progress menu. Cataclysm unlocks when you discover Martha's Vamos, and Yesteryear when you discover the Ridgeway. That makes them the best value in the game: one find, one working cheat.

Tier 3: the smallest counters

Next come challenges with low targets. Exploding Balls needs 20 enemies hit with explosive damage. No Fall Damage needs 20 survived falls of 40 feet or more. Vehicle Force needs 25 vehicle kills, and Raise Heat Level needs 25 escapes from a three-star wanted level. Each of the eight challenge tracks also hands over a vehicle spawn at just five completed challenges, including the Maibatsu Boomazoom.

Tier 4: time, not skill

A few cheats only need time with your partner. Golden Hour needs four hours holding hands and Dollhouse eight hours together. Neither asks for skill. They build up as you play, especially if you bring your partner along for everything else.

What to leave for later

The slow ones are the 100-count grinds (workouts, fish, grenades, Molotovs, stolen police vehicles), the full challenge tracks, and anything needing every collectible in a set. Start their counters early in the Tracker, but do not wait on them.

Cheat-by-cheat notes

$250,000

Adds $250,000 to your balance instantly. To unlock it, finish the main story. The input is 8 presses: Up, Right, Down, Left, Up, Left, Down, Right. Use it on a throwaway save to test-drive expensive purchases before committing real story money.

Unlimited Focus

Your Focus meter never drains. To unlock it, finish the main story. The input is 10 presses: Down, Up, Up, Down, Up, Down, Up, Down, Up, Right. Pairs well with the shooting-range medal grind if you want to practise aim without a meter running out.

Attack Force

Bullets and melee hits launch targets on impact. To unlock it, finish the main story. The input is 9 presses: Down, Up, Left, Left, Right, Down, Down, Up, Down. It shares its first 6 or more inputs with Exploding Balls, Vehicle Force, Exploding Bottles, so slow down at the end. Shares its first five inputs with Exploding Balls and Vehicle Force.

Cycle Weather

Cycles through weather conditions. To unlock it, finish the main story. A few guides say you must reveal the whole map instead, so sources differ. The input is 7 presses: Down, Right, Up, Down, Right, Up, Right. It shares its first 6 or more inputs with Move Time Forward, Pause Time of Day, so slow down at the end. Enter it repeatedly to step through conditions until you reach the one you want.

MTL Pumper

Spawns an MTL Pumper. To unlock it, discover the MTL Pumper in the Progress menu. The input is 8 presses: Left, Up, Right, Down, Left, Right, Right, Up. It shares its first 5 or more inputs with Interstate Ambulance, Airboat 09, Bravado Banshee, Tuff BMX, Maibatsu Boomazoom, Nagasaki Carbon RS, '65 Vapid Dominator, Progen Emerus, Inflatable Flamingo, Vapid Stanier G2 Taxi, Hauners P-996 Lazer, HVY Bulldozer, Lapwing Mallard, Brimstone Golfer, Hauners Rhino Tank, Maibatsu Sanchez, Nagasaki Sea Sparrow, Speeder 700 GT, Truffade Thrax, Western Zombie Bobber, so slow down at the end. Differs from HVY Bulldozer only on the last input (Up).

HVY Bulldozer

Spawns an HVY Bulldozer. To unlock it, discover the HVY Bulldozer in the Progress menu. The input is 8 presses: Left, Up, Right, Down, Left, Right, Right, Down. It shares its first 5 or more inputs with Interstate Ambulance, Airboat 09, Bravado Banshee, Tuff BMX, Maibatsu Boomazoom, Nagasaki Carbon RS, '65 Vapid Dominator, Progen Emerus, Inflatable Flamingo, MTL Pumper, Vapid Stanier G2 Taxi, Hauners P-996 Lazer, Lapwing Mallard, Brimstone Golfer, Hauners Rhino Tank, Maibatsu Sanchez, Nagasaki Sea Sparrow, Speeder 700 GT, Truffade Thrax, Western Zombie Bobber, so slow down at the end. Ends with Down, where the MTL Pumper ends with Up.

Cataclysm

Fills the world with murky smoke. To unlock it, discover Martha's Vamos in the Progress menu. The input is 10 presses: Down, Down, Up, Right, Right, Up, Left, Left, Right, Right. It shares its first 8 or more inputs with Golden Hour, Powdered Sugar, Pinhole, Neon Jungle, Yesteryear, Videotape, Dark Mirror, Migraine, Dollhouse, Bubblegum, Fast Forward, Fisheye, Faded Glory, Pixel Art, Hellscape, so slow down at the end. Filter codes start Down, Down, Up, Right, Right, Up, Left, Left. Only the last two inputs change.

Exploding Balls

Sports balls explode on impact. To unlock it, hit 20 enemies with explosive damage. The input is 9 presses: Down, Up, Left, Left, Right, Down, Down, Up, Right. It shares its first 6 or more inputs with Attack Force, Vehicle Force, Exploding Bottles, so slow down at the end. The same counter also unlocks Explosive Shotgun (Mason & Keen Shortstraw) at 100. Identical to Attack Force except the last input is Right.

Vehicle Force

Vehicle collisions launch targets. To unlock it, kill 25 enemies with a vehicle (civilians count). The input is 9 presses: Down, Up, Left, Left, Right, Down, Left, Up, Down. It shares its first 6 or more inputs with Attack Force, Exploding Balls, Exploding Bottles, so slow down at the end. The seventh input is Left, which is the one difference from Attack Force.

Raise Heat Level

Raises your heat level across the map. To unlock it, lose a three-star wanted level 25 times. The input is 7 presses: Left, Down, Right, Down, Up, Down, Up. It shares its first 5 or more inputs with Raise Wanted Level, so slow down at the end. The same counter also unlocks Raise Wanted Level at 50. Only the sixth input differs from Raise Wanted Level (Down here, Right there). Double-check before you enter it.

Maibatsu Boomazoom

Spawns a Maibatsu Boomazoom where you stand. To unlock it, complete five shopping challenges. The input is 8 presses: Left, Up, Right, Down, Left, Left, Up, Left. It shares its first 5 or more inputs with Interstate Ambulance, Airboat 09, Bravado Banshee, Tuff BMX, Nagasaki Carbon RS, '65 Vapid Dominator, Progen Emerus, Inflatable Flamingo, MTL Pumper, Vapid Stanier G2 Taxi, Hauners P-996 Lazer, HVY Bulldozer, Lapwing Mallard, Brimstone Golfer, Hauners Rhino Tank, Maibatsu Sanchez, Nagasaki Sea Sparrow, Speeder 700 GT, Truffade Thrax, Western Zombie Bobber, so slow down at the end. Vehicle spawn codes start Left, Up, Right, Down. Learn that opener once.

Dollhouse

Applies a vivid filter with a narrow aspect ratio. To unlock it, spend eight hours with your partner. The input is 10 presses: Down, Down, Up, Right, Right, Up, Left, Left, Down, Right. It shares its first 8 or more inputs with Golden Hour, Powdered Sugar, Pinhole, Neon Jungle, Yesteryear, Cataclysm, Videotape, Dark Mirror, Migraine, Bubblegum, Fast Forward, Fisheye, Faded Glory, Pixel Art, Hellscape, so slow down at the end. Ends Down, Right. Cataclysm ends Right, Right.

Which GTA 6 cheats unlock first?
