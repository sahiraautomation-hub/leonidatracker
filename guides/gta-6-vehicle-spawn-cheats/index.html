GTA 6 Vehicle Spawn Cheats: All 21 Vehicles and How to Unlock Them

CheatsBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

Vehicle spawn cheats are the biggest group in GTA 6, with 21 entries. Most are earned through the challenge tracks in the Progress menu, and three come from discovering a vehicle.

How the challenge tracks work

Challenges are split into eight tracks: survival, combat, fitness, shopping, exploration, vehicle, sports and criminal. Each track gives one vehicle at five completed challenges and a second vehicle when you finish the whole track. Completing every challenge in the game unlocks the Hauners P-996 Lazer.

Known spawn codes

Three spawn codes are known so far. All open with Left, Up, Right, Down, Left. The Maibatsu Boomazoom then goes Left, Up, Left. The MTL Pumper goes Right, Right, Up, and the HVY Bulldozer goes Right, Right, Down.

Quick wins

The five-challenge rewards are the fastest: Airboat 09 (survival), Speeder 700 GT (combat), Tuff BMX (fitness), Boomazoom (shopping), Sea Sparrow (exploration), Lapwing Mallard (vehicle), Stanier taxi (sports) and Sanchez (criminal). The Inflatable Flamingo is a fun outlier that needs 50 Cocaine Flamingos destroyed. Track all eight track counters in the Tracker.

Fast cars without cheats

Two spawn cheats, the Truffade Thrax and Progen Emerus, are also the two fastest cars in GTA 6. To own cars during the story, see how to keep and register cars.

Frequently asked questions

Which vehicle cheat is hardest to unlock?

The Hauners P-996 Lazer, which needs every challenge in the game.

Can I spawn vehicles in the water?

The Airboat 09, Inflatable Flamingo and Sea Sparrow are useful around water, but spawn behaviour depends on where you stand.

How do Progress menu vehicles unlock?

The Interstate Ambulance, MTL Pumper and HVY Bulldozer unlock once the vehicle is discovered and appears in the Progress menu.
