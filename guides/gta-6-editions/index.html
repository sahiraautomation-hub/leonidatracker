GTA 6 Editions Compared: Standard, Ultimate and Pre-Order Bonuses

BasicsBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·4 min read

GTA 6 is sold in two editions on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S: Standard and Ultimate. The Ultimate Edition is the base game plus a set of extra vehicles, weapons, outfits, shops and activities. This page lists what Rockstar announced for each edition, and what came with pre-orders before the game launched on 19 November 2026.

The two editions and their prices

Rockstar announced the prices in June 2026. In the United States the Standard Edition costs $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition costs $99.99. UK prices were reported as £69.99 and £89.99. Check your console store for the price in your region.

Both editions are for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only. There is no PC version yet. The release date and platforms guide covers that in more detail.

There is no disc

GTA 6 is a digital game on both consoles. The boxed edition sold in shops holds a download code, not a disc. Boxed copies went on sale on 12 November 2026, a week before launch, so that buyers could redeem the code and pre-load. Digital pre-loading began on the same day.

What the Ultimate Edition adds

  • Vehicles: the '95 Grotti Cheetah sports car, the '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy with its own garage, the Squalo boat, and extra vehicles at Jason's safehouse, including a Dinka Enduro motorcycle and a Crest kayak.
  • Vehicle build: the Ganado Retro Build, a set of retro modifications for Jason's Vapid Ganado pickup.
  • Weapons: Hawk & Little Morgan revolvers for Jason and Lucia, plus two personalised pistols, a Girardi ES9 for Jason and a Klose K17 for Lucia.
  • Looks: the Vice City Style outfits and tattoos, and the Goodtime Gear clothing set.
  • Shops: the mod shops Rideout Customs and One-Eyed Willie's, the Stock 305 clothing store, Sara's Unisex Salon and Electric Fang Tattoo.
  • Activities: the PTT Youngin$ Illegal Goods Store, a gang compound, and the Classic Car Collection, a set of project cars from a character named Wyman.

Rockstar listed 16 bonuses for the Ultimate Edition. They are not all handed over at the start. Coverage of the announcement says they open up at different points as the story moves on.

The two mod shops and the Stock 305 clothing store are described as open only to Ultimate Edition owners. The salon and the tattoo shop were reported the same way.

Pre-order bonus: the Vintage Vice City Pack

  • A '55 Vapid Stanier saloon, kept in the Shore Court garage. The garage has a weapon locker and a drop-off for stolen goods you want to fence.
  • Vintage outfits and hairstyles for Jason and Lucia.
  • A tropical pattern for most guns, a nod to Tommy Vercetti's shirt from GTA: Vice City.

Every pre-order came with the Vintage Vice City Pack, whichever edition you chose. Pre-orders closed when the game launched. We have not been able to confirm whether the pack will be sold separately, so treat any claim about that as unconfirmed.

Digital pre-orders on the PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store also included one free month of GTA+, Rockstar's subscription. It renews at the normal price unless you cancel it. Boxed pre-orders were reported not to include the free month.

Upgrading from Standard to Ultimate

The gap between the two editions is $20. Before launch, outlets expected a separate Ultimate upgrade for Standard owners at about that price, sold through the PlayStation Store and Microsoft Store and open to boxed copies too. We have not confirmed the current store price, so check it on your console before you buy.

Which edition should you pick?

The Standard Edition is the base game at the lower price. The Ultimate Edition suits players who want the extra cars, weapons and shops from the list above. We have not confirmed whether any Ultimate-only shop or item counts towards 100% completion or a trophy, so nothing on this site assumes that it does.

Frequently asked questions

How much does GTA 6 cost?

In the US, $79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition.

Does GTA 6 come on a disc?

No. Boxed copies contain a download code instead of a disc.

What was the GTA 6 pre-order bonus?

The Vintage Vice City Pack with every pre-order, plus one free month of GTA+ for digital pre-orders on the PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store.

What is in the GTA 6 Ultimate Edition?

The base game plus 16 bonuses: extra vehicles, weapons, outfits, five shops and two activities.
