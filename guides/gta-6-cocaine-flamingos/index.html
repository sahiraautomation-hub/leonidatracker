GTA 6 Cocaine Flamingos: All 125, Rewards and 100% Requirements

CollectiblesBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

Cocaine Flamingos are one of GTA 6's stranger collectibles. There are 125 hidden across Leonida, and you destroy them by shooting them.

How they work

Shoot a Cocaine Flamingo to destroy it and it drops cocaine packages. Each one destroyed adds to your total in the Progress menu.

Flamingo counter
ItemTargetNotes
Cocaine Flamingos destroyed 125 Shoot them to destroy. 50 unlocks the Inflatable Flamingo cheat.

Rewards

  • 50 destroyed unlocks the Inflatable Flamingo vehicle spawn cheat.
  • 100 destroyed is the community-reported requirement for 100% completion.
  • 125 is the full count.

Hunting efficiently

Combine flamingo hunting with WAiNK scanning while you sweep each region. Update your count in the Tracker after each outing. The same counter powers the Inflatable Flamingo row in the Cheat Unlock Planner.

Pacing the hunt

The two milestones that matter are 50 and 100. Fifty destroyed is 40% of the full count and unlocks the Inflatable Flamingo. One hundred is 80% and is the community-reported 100% requirement. If you split the hunt across Leonida's six regions, roughly 17 per region gets you to 100. That is a planning target, not a claim about how flamingos are distributed.

Recording finds

Flamingos are easy to lose count of because they are destroyed rather than picked up. Tap the plus button in the Tracker right after each one, and check your total against the Progress menu at the end of a session.

Item-by-item notes
