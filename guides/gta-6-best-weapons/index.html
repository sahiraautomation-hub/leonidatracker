The Best Weapons in GTA 6 for Every Class

WeaponsBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

GTA 6 has 34 standard guns, plus named and modded variants you can find in the world. You do not need most of them. A handful cover every situation, and the best first purchases are cheap. Stats below are the in-game ratings out of 10.

What to buy first

Two guns are discounted during the Chapter 1 mission Gear Up, and both are worth taking. The Moreland 850 shotgun drops from $700 to $350, and the Hawk & Little Mediator tactical rifle drops from $900 to $450. Pick whichever suits you and come back for the other later.

Best all-round gun: Duke 556

If you buy one rifle, make it the Duke 556. It costs $1,450, holds 30 rounds and accepts a wide range of attachments. Nearly every launch guide names it the best general-purpose weapon, and it stays useful for the whole story.

Best sidearm

The Girardi ES9 is the most recommended everyday pistol because it is accurate and easy to handle. For raw damage, the Fairview Kalinga .50 pistol and the Scorpio Akulus revolver both rate 7.0. The Akulus costs $3,000.

Best close-range weapons

Two shotguns top the damage chart at 8.0: the Shrewsbury Drifter at $1,150 and the Mason & Keen Shortstraw at $1,625. Both hold only two shells, and the Drifter has no modification slots. The Moreland 850 does less damage per shot but is more forgiving. For a fast-firing option, the Vom Feuer VF5 submachine gun costs $2,325.

Best long-range weapons

The Fairview FF-50 is the hardest-hitting sniper rifle at 8.5 damage. It costs $3,975 and holds five rounds. The Fortune & Son Laird 350 is the most accurate gun in the game at 8.8 accuracy, which makes it the easier rifle to land shots with.

Heavy weapons

The ADB M350 is the top machine gun at 6.5 damage. The two launchers hit hardest of all: the Hauners H-AT rocket launcher at 9.6 damage for $16,000, and the Deever Tech GLR40 grenade launcher at 9.2 for $15,500.

Weapons and cheats

Two of these guns also appear in the cheat list. Blowing up 100 vehicles unlocks the Hauners H-AT cheat, and hitting 100 enemies with explosive damage unlocks an explosive Mason & Keen Shortstraw. See combat cheats. For where to buy everything and how to keep it, read the gun shop guide.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best gun in GTA 6?

The Duke 556 assault rifle is the best all-round weapon. It costs $1,450, holds 30 rounds and takes many attachments.

What weapon should I buy first in GTA 6?

The Moreland 850 shotgun or the Hawk & Little Mediator rifle. Both are half price during the Chapter 1 mission Gear Up.

What is the best sniper rifle in GTA 6?

The Fairview FF-50, with a damage rating of 8.5. It costs $3,975.
