GTA 6 Body Armor Cheat: Code and 100-Workout Unlock

CheatsBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

Body Armor adds armor straight to your loadout. It is a player cheat, but instead of the story it asks for 100 completed workouts.

The code

Enter Down, Up, Left, Up, Up, Up, Up, Up, Down, Down on the phone while holding L1 and pressing R1 on PS5, or LB and RB on Xbox. The five Ups in a row are the tricky part. Count them out loud or tap in a steady beat.

Getting to 100 workouts

Workouts are counted in total, so short sessions add up. Fitness challenges sit on their own challenge track, so time spent working out can push your fitness challenge count toward the Tuff BMX and '65 Vapid Dominator spawn cheats at the same time. Log workouts in the Tracker at the end of each session.

Is there a health cheat?

No health or invincibility cheat appears in the current list of 61. Body Armor is the closest survival cheat, alongside No Fall Damage.

Cheat-by-cheat notes

Body Armor

Adds body armor to your loadout. To unlock it, complete 100 workouts. The input is 10 presses: Down, Up, Left, Up, Up, Up, Up, Up, Down, Down. The five Up presses in the middle are the easy place to miscount. Tap them in a steady rhythm.

No Fall Damage

Makes you immune to fall damage. To unlock it, survive a fall of 40 feet or more 20 times. The code has not been confirmed yet. Work on the challenge now so it is ready when the code is published. Save before attempting high falls so a failed landing does not cost progress.
