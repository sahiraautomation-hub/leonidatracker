GTA 6 Jewelry Store Robberies: How They Work and What They Pay

MoneyBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

Jewelry stores are the best money in the middle of GTA 6. They unlock in Chapter 3, pay far more than ordinary shops, and reward a quiet approach. They also draw a hard police response if you get it wrong.

When they unlock

Jewelry store jobs open in Chapter 3, once the story reaches Vice City and introduces the fence who buys the goods. From that point jewelry stores appear as robbery targets, each with a security level and a take value on the map.

Get the Snap Gun first

The Snap Gun is a lock-picking tool sold by a fence in Vice City. It opens doors without setting off the alarm, which is what makes a quiet jewelry job possible. Without it you are forcing your way in, and forced entry triggers the alarm.

The method

  1. Check the store's security level and take value on the map before you commit.
  2. Enter quietly with the Snap Gun, or control the staff by threatening them so nobody raises the alarm.
  3. Go to the back room and switch off the security system.
  4. Smash the display cases and take the jewelry.
  5. Crack the back-room safe. Clearing the cases alone is reported not to count as a full robbery.
  6. Destroy the camera footage, leave, and take the goods to your fence.

Payout

A jewelry store is reported to pay $10,000 to $25,000. Players also report that stores marked with a diamond icon are worth more than those marked with a ring. Hitting several stores in one run before the police catch up is the fastest way to stack money in Chapter 3.

The Sneakthief trophy

Robbing a jewelry store without being detected earns Sneakthief, a Bronze trophy. The Snap Gun is what makes this realistic. It is listed with the other 54 in the trophy guide.

What comes next

Jewelry stores carry you through Chapter 3 alongside Randy's Wish List. Late in Chapter 4, bank vaults take over as the top earner.

When can you rob jewelry stores in GTA 6?

From Chapter 3, after the story introduces the Vice City fence who buys jewelry.

Launch guides report $10,000 to $25,000 per store.

What is the Snap Gun in GTA 6?

A lock-picking tool bought from a fence in Vice City. It opens doors without triggering the alarm.
