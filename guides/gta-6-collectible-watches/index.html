GTA 6 Collectible Watches: Move Time Forward and Pause Time Cheats

CollectiblesBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

Watches are hidden around Leonida, and they are the key to GTA 6's two time-of-day cheats.

Rewards

  • 10 watches unlock Move Time Forward.
  • 20 watches unlock Pause Time of Day.

At least 20 watches exist, so the second cheat is reachable without a full sweep of the map.

Why the time cheats are useful

Pause Time of Day and Cycle Weather together give you full control of the light. That is ideal for screenshots and for activities that are easier in daylight. Remember that cheats disable saving, so set up shots on a backup save.

Tracking

One counter in the Tracker covers both cheats. The Cheat Unlock Planner shows how many more you need for each.

Cheat-by-cheat notes

Cycle Weather

Cycles through weather conditions. To unlock it, finish the main story. A few guides say you must reveal the whole map instead, so sources differ. The input is 7 presses: Down, Right, Up, Down, Right, Up, Right. It shares its first 6 or more inputs with Move Time Forward, so slow down at the end. Enter it repeatedly to step through conditions until you reach the one you want.

Move Time Forward

Moves the time of day forward. To unlock it, find 10 collectible watches. The input is 7 presses: Down, Right, Up, Down, Right, Up, Up. It shares its first 6 or more inputs with Cycle Weather, so slow down at the end. The same counter also unlocks Pause Time of Day at 20. Same as Cycle Weather until the last press, which is Up here. The watch counter also unlocks Pause Time of Day at 20.

Pause Time of Day

Freezes the time of day. To unlock it, find 20 collectible watches. The code has not been confirmed yet. Work on the challenge now so it is ready when the code is published. The same counter also unlocks Move Time Forward at 10. Combine with Cycle Weather for screenshots in exactly the light you want.

Frequently asked questions

How many watches unlock Pause Time of Day?

20.

Do the time cheats have known codes?

One does. Move Time Forward is Down, Right, Up, Down, Right, Up, Up, entered on the phone like any other cheat. The Pause Time of Day code has not been found yet.

Is there a cheat to change the weather?

Yes, Cycle Weather. Most guides list it as a story unlock, though a few say you must reveal the whole map.
