GTA 6 Controls for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S

BasicsBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

GTA 6 changes a few controls that series veterans know by heart. The biggest is running: you no longer tap a face button to sprint. This page lists the default layout for both consoles and explains the changes worth knowing.

What changed from GTA 5

  • Running is on the left stick. Click L3 to run, and click again to sprint. There is no tap-to-sprint.
  • Crouch is on Circle or B. Hold it to rest.
  • The D-pad does double duty. A tap and a hold do different things on every direction.
  • Focus is new. Click both sticks to slow time in a fight or highlight things around you.

Switching characters

Once the story unlocks it, hold Right on the D-pad to open the partner menu, then press R1 on PS5 or RB on Xbox to switch between Jason and Lucia. More in fast travel and switching.

Driving

  • Accelerate and brake with the triggers: R2 and L2, or RT and LT.
  • Handbrake: Cross or A.
  • Shoot from the vehicle: R1 or RB.
  • Exit the vehicle: Triangle or Y.

Control schemes

Settings offers three schemes. Default is the layout above. Classic moves Run back to Cross or A and puts Jump on Square or X, which will feel familiar from older games. Left-Handed mirrors the sticks, shoulders and triggers.

Entering cheats

Cheats use these same inputs: Up on the D-pad for the phone, then hold L1 and press R1 on PS5, or LB and RB on Xbox, and enter the arrows. See how to enter cheats.

Frequently asked questions

How do you run in GTA 6?

Click the left stick (L3). Click again to sprint. On the Classic scheme, running is on Cross or A.

How do you switch characters in GTA 6?

Hold Right on the D-pad and press R1 on PS5 or RB on Xbox, once the story has unlocked switching.

Can you change the controls in GTA 6?

Yes. Settings offers Default, Classic and Left-Handed control schemes.
