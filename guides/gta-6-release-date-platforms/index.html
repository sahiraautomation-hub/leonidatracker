GTA 6 Release Date, Platforms and PC Version

WorldBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

GTA 6 launched on Thursday, 19 November 2026 for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, after two delays from its original autumn 2025 window.

The road to launch

Rockstar first targeted autumn 2025, then moved to 26 May 2026, then announced in November 2025 that the game would arrive on 19 November 2026. That date held.

Pre-load and boxed copies

Digital pre-orders could pre-load from local midnight on 12 November 2026. Boxed copies contain a download code rather than a disc, and those codes also supported pre-loading once redeemed.

Is GTA 6 on PC?

Not at launch. GTA 6 is on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, and Rockstar has not announced a PC release date. Past Rockstar games reached PC after console, but nothing is confirmed for GTA 6.

What the platforms mean for cheats and trophies

Because GTA 6 is console-only for now, every cheat in the game uses the same D-pad sequence on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Only the shoulder buttons you hold change: L1 and R1 on PS5, LB and RB on Xbox. Trophies and achievements follow the same rule on both: using a cheat switches them off for that session. See how to enter cheats for the full input.

A sensible first week

Most of the long-tail goals in GTA 6, such as 100 workouts, 100 fish or 125 Cocaine Flamingos, are easier when counted from day one than reconstructed later. Start a Tracker save in your first session, play the story at your own pace, and log counters as you go. Cheats only unlock after the 91-mission story anyway, so there is no reason to rush.
