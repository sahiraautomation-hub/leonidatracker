GTA 6 Meditation Spots: All 10 Locations

CollectiblesBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·5 min read

Quick answer: GTA 6 has 10 meditation spots that add up to 18 meditations. One is a lesson at Jason's house in Chapter 1. Eight are out in the open world and count once each for Jason and Lucia. The last one is on top of Mount Kalaga and only appears when the rest are done. Guides report that 15 of the 18 are enough for 100% completion. Nothing on this page spoils the story.

How the 18 meditations add up

Guides that list every spot agree on 10 locations. The first is a lesson with Lucia at Jason's house, and it counts once. The next eight are open-world spots. Each of those counts twice, once when Jason meditates there and once when Lucia does. The tenth, called Ascent, counts once. That gives 1 + 16 + 1 = 18.

One guide numbers them as 18 spots instead of 10 places. It describes the same locations in the same order, so the two ways of counting match.

Learning to meditate in Chapter 1

Meditation opens in Chapter 1. After the mission Down to Earth, Lucia says she is going to meditate at Jason's house. Guides say to aim at her with L2 on PS5 or LT on Xbox and choose Accept to join her. Finishing the lesson counts as your first meditation.

The lesson is only on offer during Chapter 1, and guides differ on what happens if you skip it. One early trophy guide says the trophy is then out of reach. A more detailed guide reports that the lesson is not needed for the trophy or for 100%, and that a replacement lesson is marked on the map from Chapter 2 for players who missed it. The safe choice is to do it in Chapter 1. The Chapter 1 checklist lists everything else that closes with the chapter.

All 10 meditation spots

The names are the ones guides use for each spot. Each place in the table is supported by at least two guides. The county is left out where guides give different ones.

One guide warns that the Waterfall spot is on a rock in the river and is awkward to reach on foot. It recommends a helicopter for that spot, for the sandbar and for the mountain top.

How to find spots on the map

You do not need to memorise the table. Guides report that a spot shows up as a purple icon on the map once you are close to it, and that the Exploration filter on the map has an entry called Meditate Around Leonida. Your running total is under Progress, then Collections, Exploration and Meditations. The Progress menu guide explains the other cards.

Each open-world spot has to be visited by both characters to earn both of its meditations. The full run is easiest once you can switch between Jason and Lucia, which opens in Chapter 2. One trophy guide suggests waiting until Chapter 3, when more of the map is easy to reach.

Ascent: the last spot

Every guide agrees on Ascent. It is on the summit of Mount Kalaga in Lummox County, and it does not appear on the map until the other meditations are finished. One guide adds that it takes a short while to show up after the last of them, so do not worry if the icon is not there at once. Finishing Ascent completes the set.

What meditating gets you

  • Positive Mental Attitude. A Bronze trophy for meditating at every spot in Leonida. See the trophy list.
  • 100% completion. Three guides report that 15 of the 18 meditations are enough. See the 100% checklist.
  • Fast Forward. The Fast Forward filter cheat unlocks after 50 meditations. Repeats count, so you will go back to spots you have already used. The fishing and meditation guide covers the pacing.
  • Focus. Meditation is one of the activities that raises the Focus stat.

Frequently asked questions

How many meditation spots are in GTA 6?

10 locations that add up to 18 meditations. Eight of the locations count twice, once for Jason and once for Lucia.

Where is the last meditation spot in GTA 6?

On the summit of Mount Kalaga in Lummox County. It is called Ascent, and it only appears once the other meditations are done.

Can you miss a meditation spot in GTA 6?

Only the Chapter 1 lesson at Jason's house can be missed. Recent guides report that it is not needed for the trophy or for 100%, but one early guide says it is, so do it if you can.

Do you need all 18 meditations for 100%?

Guides report that 15 of the 18 are enough for 100% completion. The Positive Mental Attitude trophy asks for every spot.
