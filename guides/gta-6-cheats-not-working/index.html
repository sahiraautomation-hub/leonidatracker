GTA 6 Cheats Not Working? Seven Causes and How to Fix Each One

CheatsBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·5 min read

If a GTA 6 cheat code does nothing when you enter it, the game is almost never broken. In nearly every case one of seven things is going on, and most take seconds to check. Work through them in order.

1. You have not finished the story

This is the most common cause. Every cheat in GTA 6 stays locked until the main story is complete. Until the credits roll, the Cheats list in the Progress menu is not available and no code will register, no matter how accurately you enter it. If you are partway through the 91 missions, cheats are simply not available yet.

2. That cheat's own challenge is not done

Finishing the story unlocks only four cheats: $250,000, Unlimited Focus, Attack Force and Cycle Weather. Each of the other 57 has its own challenge. Body Armor needs 100 workouts, Max Ammo needs 25 shooting-range medals, and so on. Open the Progress menu and choose Cheats to see which ones you have actually earned. If the cheat is not listed as unlocked, its code will not work. The cheats database shows every requirement, and the Cheat Unlock Planner shows how far you are from each.

3. The phone is not out

Codes are entered through the in-game phone. Press Up on the D-pad first to bring it out. If you press the arrows without the phone out, the game reads them as normal controls, not as a cheat.

4. You let go of the shoulder buttons

On PS5, hold L1 and press R1. On Xbox, hold LB and press RB. Then keep holding while you enter the whole D-pad sequence. Releasing early cancels the input. If your hand position makes that awkward, rest the controller on your lap and use your other hand's thumb for the D-pad.

5. You miscounted a repeated press

Several codes repeat a direction. Body Armor has five Ups in a row. Infinite Ammo ends with three Downs. Unlimited Focus is nine Down and Up presses before a final Right. Tap in a steady rhythm and count out loud. Rushing is how one press gets dropped or doubled.

6. You entered a look-alike code

Many GTA 6 codes differ by a single press. Raise Heat Level and Raise Wanted Level are identical except for the sixth input. Infinite Ammo and No Reload differ only on the last press. The MTL Pumper and HVY Bulldozer also differ only at the end. If a different cheat activates, or nothing does, compare your input with its sibling in the table below.

7. The code has not been found yet

Only 18 of the 61 codes have been confirmed so far. If you are trying a code for a cheat such as Repair Vehicle, Parachute or Incendiary Ammo, any sequence you found online is unverified. This site lists a code only after it has been confirmed by more than one independent source, and marks the rest clearly.

Still stuck?

Restart the game and try the easiest code first: $250,000 (Up, Right, Down, Left, Up, Left, Down, Right). If that works, your input method is fine and the issue is the specific cheat's unlock. Remember that cheats disable saving and trophies for the session, so test on a backup save.

Cheat-by-cheat notes

Raise Heat Level

Raises your heat level across the map. To unlock it, lose a three-star wanted level 25 times. The input is 7 presses: Left, Down, Right, Down, Up, Down, Up. It shares its first 5 or more inputs with Raise Wanted Level, so slow down at the end. The same counter also unlocks Raise Wanted Level at 50. Only the sixth input differs from Raise Wanted Level (Down here, Right there). Double-check before you enter it.

Raise Wanted Level

Raises your wanted level. To unlock it, lose a three-star wanted level 50 times. The input is 7 presses: Left, Down, Right, Down, Up, Right, Up. It shares its first 5 or more inputs with Raise Heat Level, so slow down at the end. The same counter also unlocks Raise Heat Level at 25. You unlock Raise Heat Level halfway through this challenge, so both come from the same grind.

Infinite Ammo

Your ammo never runs out. To unlock it, earn 25 silver-or-better medals at shooting ranges. The input is 10 presses: Down, Up, Right, Down, Left, Left, Up, Down, Down, Down. It shares its first 7 or more inputs with Max Ammo, No Reload, so slow down at the end. Ends Down, Down, Down. Think of it as the bottom-heavy ammo code.

No Reload

Infinite ammo with no reloading. To unlock it, earn 25 gold medals at shooting ranges. The input is 10 presses: Down, Up, Right, Down, Left, Left, Up, Down, Down, Up. It shares its first 7 or more inputs with Max Ammo, Infinite Ammo, so slow down at the end. Same as Infinite Ammo until the final press, which is Up instead of Down.

MTL Pumper

Spawns an MTL Pumper. To unlock it, discover the MTL Pumper in the Progress menu. The input is 8 presses: Left, Up, Right, Down, Left, Right, Right, Up. It shares its first 5 or more inputs with Maibatsu Boomazoom, HVY Bulldozer, so slow down at the end. Differs from HVY Bulldozer only on the last input (Up).

HVY Bulldozer

Spawns an HVY Bulldozer. To unlock it, discover the HVY Bulldozer in the Progress menu. The input is 8 presses: Left, Up, Right, Down, Left, Right, Right, Down. It shares its first 5 or more inputs with Maibatsu Boomazoom, MTL Pumper, so slow down at the end. Ends with Down, where the MTL Pumper ends with Up.

Frequently asked questions

Why is my GTA 6 cheat code not working?

Most often because the main story is not finished or that cheat's own challenge is not complete. After that, the usual causes are releasing the shoulder buttons early or miscounting presses.

Do GTA 6 cheats work before finishing the story?

No. All cheats are locked until the story is complete.

How do I check which cheats I have unlocked?

Open the Progress menu after the credits and choose Cheats.
