How to Enter Cheats in GTA 6 (PS5 and Xbox Series X|S)

CheatsBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·3 min read

GTA 6 brings cheats back, but it changes the rules. You type codes through your in-game phone, every cheat has its own unlock challenge, and nothing works until the main story is finished. This page walks through the input step by step.

The four-step input

  • Press Up on the D-pad during gameplay to take out the phone.
  • On PS5, hold L1 and press R1. On Xbox Series X|S, hold LB and press RB.
  • Keep holding and enter the D-pad sequence for the cheat.
  • Watch for the on-screen confirmation that the cheat is active.

Cheat entry works the same way on both consoles. Only the shoulder buttons you hold differ. The D-pad sequence itself is identical, so a code you learn on PS5 works on Xbox without changes.

Why the Cheats list is empty at first

Every cheat is locked until the end credits roll. Once the story is done, the Cheats entry appears in the Progress menu and shows which cheats you have earned so far. Four cheats unlock with the story alone: $250,000, Unlimited Focus, Attack Force and Cycle Weather. The other 57 each need their own challenge, such as 100 workouts or 25 gold shooting-range medals.

Avoiding failed inputs

Most failed entries come from three habits. Releasing the shoulder buttons mid-sequence breaks the input, so hold them for the whole code. Miscounting repeated presses is the second, and Body Armor with its five Ups in a row is the usual culprit. The third is entering a code you have not unlocked yet. Check the cheats database or your Progress menu first so you know which inputs should work.

Make a backup save first

Activating a cheat disables saving, trophies and achievements for that session. Before experimenting, make a manual save in a separate slot so your clean progress stays untouched. Read what cheats do to trophies and saves for the full picture.

Cheat-by-cheat notes

$250,000

Adds $250,000 to your balance instantly. To unlock it, finish the main story. The input is 8 presses: Up, Right, Down, Left, Up, Left, Down, Right. Use it on a throwaway save to test-drive expensive purchases before committing real story money.

Unlimited Focus

Your Focus meter never drains. To unlock it, finish the main story. The input is 10 presses: Down, Up, Up, Down, Up, Down, Up, Down, Up, Right. Pairs well with the shooting-range medal grind if you want to practise aim without a meter running out.

Attack Force

Bullets and melee hits launch targets on impact. To unlock it, finish the main story. The input is 9 presses: Down, Up, Left, Left, Right, Down, Down, Up, Down. It shares its first 6 or more inputs with Exploding Balls, Vehicle Force, so slow down at the end. Shares its first five inputs with Exploding Balls and Vehicle Force.

Cycle Weather

Cycles through weather conditions. To unlock it, finish the main story. A few guides say you must reveal the whole map instead, so sources differ. The input is 7 presses: Down, Right, Up, Down, Right, Up, Right. It shares its first 6 or more inputs with Move Time Forward, so slow down at the end. Enter it repeatedly to step through conditions until you reach the one you want.

Frequently asked questions

Are GTA 6 cheat codes different on PS5 and Xbox?

No. The D-pad sequence is the same. Only the buttons you hold change: L1 and R1 on PS5, LB and RB on Xbox Series X|S.

Can I use cheats before finishing the story?

No. Cheats unlock only after the main story is complete, and each one also needs its own challenge.

Where do I see which cheats I have unlocked?

After the credits, open the Progress menu and choose Cheats.
