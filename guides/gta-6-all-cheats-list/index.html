Patterns that make codes easier

Cheats in the same family share an opening. The three ammo cheats begin Down, Up, Right, Down, Left, Left, Up and differ only at the end. Vehicle spawns open with Left, Up, Right, Down. The found screen filters all begin Down, Down, Up, Right, Right, Up, Left, Left. Learn the opener once and you only need to remember the last two or three inputs.

Where to start

The four story cheats need nothing beyond the credits. After that, the quickest unlocks are usually the ones with small targets: 20 explosive hits for Exploding Balls, 25 vehicle kills for Vehicle Force, and five shopping challenges for the Maibatsu Boomazoom.
