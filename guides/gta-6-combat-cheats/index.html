GTA 6 Combat Cheats: Attack Force, Explosives and Incendiary Ammo

CheatsBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·4 min read

GTA 6 has 11 combat cheats. Six have known codes so far, including three physics cheats that send targets flying.

The physics trio

Attack Force makes bullets and melee hits launch targets. Vehicle Force does the same for collisions, and Exploding Balls turns sports balls into explosives. All three begin Down, Up, Left, Left, Right, Down. Attack Force then ends Down, Up, Down. Exploding Balls ends Down, Up, Right. Vehicle Force ends Left, Up, Down.

Explosive and fire unlocks

Several combat cheats share overlapping grinds. Throwing 100 Molotovs unlocks Exploding Bottles and also burns plenty of enemies toward the 100 needed for Incendiary Ammo. Explosive hits count for both Exploding Balls at 20 and the explosive Mason & Keen Shortstraw at 100. Throwing 100 grenades unlocks All Explosive Weapons.

Plan the overlap

Work on related counters together and you will unlock several cheats in one stretch. The Cheat Unlock Planner shows which ones you are closest to.

Cheat-by-cheat notes

Max Ammo

Fills all your ammo to the maximum. To unlock it, earn 25 bronze-or-better medals at shooting ranges. The input is 10 presses: Down, Up, Right, Down, Left, Left, Up, Up, Down, Left. It shares its first 7 or more inputs with Infinite Ammo, No Reload, so slow down at the end. The three ammo cheats share their first seven inputs. Only the last three change.

Infinite Ammo

Your ammo never runs out. To unlock it, earn 25 silver-or-better medals at shooting ranges. The input is 10 presses: Down, Up, Right, Down, Left, Left, Up, Down, Down, Down. It shares its first 7 or more inputs with Max Ammo, No Reload, so slow down at the end. Ends Down, Down, Down. Think of it as the bottom-heavy ammo code.

No Reload

Infinite ammo with no reloading. To unlock it, earn 25 gold medals at shooting ranges. The input is 10 presses: Down, Up, Right, Down, Left, Left, Up, Down, Down, Up. It shares its first 7 or more inputs with Max Ammo, Infinite Ammo, so slow down at the end. Same as Infinite Ammo until the final press, which is Up instead of Down.

Attack Force

Bullets and melee hits launch targets on impact. To unlock it, finish the main story. The input is 9 presses: Down, Up, Left, Left, Right, Down, Down, Up, Down. It shares its first 6 or more inputs with Exploding Balls, Vehicle Force, so slow down at the end. Shares its first five inputs with Exploding Balls and Vehicle Force.

Exploding Balls

Sports balls explode on impact. To unlock it, hit 20 enemies with explosive damage. The input is 9 presses: Down, Up, Left, Left, Right, Down, Down, Up, Right. It shares its first 6 or more inputs with Attack Force, Vehicle Force, so slow down at the end. The same counter also unlocks Explosive Shotgun (Mason & Keen Shortstraw) at 100. Identical to Attack Force except the last input is Right.

Vehicle Force

Vehicle collisions launch targets. To unlock it, kill 25 enemies with a vehicle (civilians count). The input is 9 presses: Down, Up, Left, Left, Right, Down, Left, Up, Down. It shares its first 6 or more inputs with Attack Force, Exploding Balls, so slow down at the end. The seventh input is Left, which is the one difference from Attack Force.

All Explosive Weapons

Gives you the explosive weapons set. To unlock it, throw 100 grenades. The code has not been confirmed yet. Work on the challenge now so it is ready when the code is published. Thrown grenades count whether or not they hit anything.

Incendiary Ammo

Makes all ammo incendiary. To unlock it, burn 100 enemies with fire damage. The code has not been confirmed yet. Work on the challenge now so it is ready when the code is published. Grinding Exploding Bottles first gives you plenty of fire damage practice.

Exploding Bottles

Thrown bottles explode. To unlock it, throw 100 Molotov cocktails. The code has not been confirmed yet. Work on the challenge now so it is ready when the code is published. Molotov throws also push you toward the Incendiary Ammo challenge.

Explosive Shotgun (Mason & Keen Shortstraw)

Gives an explosive Mason & Keen Shortstraw shotgun. To unlock it, hit 100 enemies with explosive damage. The code has not been confirmed yet. Work on the challenge now so it is ready when the code is published. The same counter also unlocks Exploding Balls at 20. Shares a counter with Exploding Balls, which unlocks at 20.

Hauners H-AT

Combat cheat tied to the Hauners H-AT. Exact effect not yet confirmed. To unlock it, blow up 100 vehicles. The code has not been confirmed yet. Work on the challenge now so it is ready when the code is published. Destroyed vehicles in any situation count, including during wanted-level chases.
