GTA 6 Underwater Discoveries: All 33 and 100% Completion

CollectiblesBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

There are 33 underwater discoveries in GTA 6, and unlike cards or flamingos, every single one is required for 100%.

Why they matter

Most collectibles in GTA 6 give you some slack for 100%. Underwater discoveries do not. All 33 are required, so they are worth tracking carefully.

Underwater counter
ItemTargetNotes
Underwater Discoveries 33 All 33 are needed for 100%.

Where they come from

The discoveries are linked to Kenton's treasure diving questline. Kenton appears in Chapter 1 through the Island Dreams mission, so the coastline around the Leonida Keys is a natural place to begin.

Diving tips

Take a boat out and anchor near a search area instead of swimming long distances from shore. Count each find in the Tracker as soon as you surface.

Why all 33 changes your plan

Most 100% lists let you skip a few entries. This one does not, which makes it the list most likely to leave you stuck at 99%. Treat it as the first collectible to finish rather than the last. A single missing discovery is far harder to find once you have forgotten which areas you have already searched.

Keep a search log

Use the counter in the Tracker for the total, and tick off each region in the Regions list once you are confident you have searched its waters. If your count stalls short of 33, the unticked regions tell you where to look again. The 100% gap check flags this requirement until it reads 33 of 33.
