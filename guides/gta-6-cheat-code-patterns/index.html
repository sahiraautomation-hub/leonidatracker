How to Memorise GTA 6 Cheat Codes Using Their Patterns

CheatsBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·3 min read

GTA 6's D-pad codes look random until you line them up. Cheats in the same family share the same opening inputs and change only at the end. Learn a handful of openers and a couple of shapes, and all 61 codes become easy to keep in your head.

The ammo opener

Max Ammo, Infinite Ammo and No Reload all begin Down, Up, Right, Down, Left, Left, Up. Only the last three presses change. Max Ammo ends Up, Down, Left. Infinite Ammo ends Down, Down, Down. No Reload ends Down, Down, Up. Memory hook: Infinite keeps going down forever, and No Reload bounces back up.

Incendiary Ammo and the Explosive Shotgun use a cousin of that opener: Down, Up, Right, Down, Left, Down, Up, Right, Down. The last press is Down for Incendiary Ammo and Left for the shotgun.

The force opener

Attack Force, Exploding Balls and Vehicle Force all begin Down, Up, Left, Left, Right, Down. Attack Force ends Down, Up, Down. Exploding Balls is the same but its final press is Right. Vehicle Force swaps the seventh press to Left, giving Left, Up, Down.

Exploding Bottles belongs to the same family. It ends Down, Up, Left.

The vehicle opener

Every vehicle spawn begins Left, Up, Right, Down, Left, a clockwise circle that closes back on Left. Three more presses pick the vehicle, so all 21 codes are eight inputs long. The Boomazoom ends Left, Up, Left. The MTL Pumper ends Right, Right, Up, the HVY Bulldozer Right, Right, Down and the Interstate Ambulance Right, Right, Right. Memory hook: Up for the Pumper, Down for the Bulldozer, like a blade dropping to the ground, and straight on for the Ambulance. The Lapwing Mallard ends Up, Up, Up and the Speeder 700 GT ends Down, Up, Up. The table below has the other 15 endings.

The filter opener

All 16 screen filters begin Down, Down, Up, Right, Right, Up, Left, Left. Only the final two presses differ, and the 16 filters use every possible pair once. Endings that start with Up: Golden Hour (Up, Up), Powdered Sugar (Up, Right), Pinhole (Up, Down), Neon Jungle (Up, Left). Endings that start with Right: Yesteryear (Right, Up), Cataclysm (Right, Right), Videotape (Right, Down), Dark Mirror (Right, Left). Endings that start with Down: Migraine (Down, Up), Dollhouse (Down, Right), Bubblegum (Down, Down), Fast Forward (Down, Left). Endings that start with Left: Fisheye (Left, Up), Faded Glory (Left, Right), Pixel Art (Left, Down), Hellscape (Left, Left). Learn them four at a time.

The shapes

The $250,000 code is two circles: clockwise (Up, Right, Down, Left) and then counter-clockwise (Up, Left, Down, Right). Unlimited Focus is a seesaw: Down, Up, Up, then Down, Up three times, ending with Right. Cycle Weather repeats Down, Right, Up twice and finishes with Right. Move Time Forward is the same code with Up as the final press. The two wanted codes begin Left, Down, Right, Down, Up and split on the sixth press: Down for heat, Right for wanted.

Drunk is a stumble built from one pair. Left, Right comes three times, with one Up before it, then two Ups, then one: Up, Left, Right, then Up, Up, Left, Right, then Up, Left, Right.

A few more work the same way. Pause Time of Day is the Cycle Weather code with Left as the final press. Repair Vehicle and Unlimited Nitrous both begin Right, Down, Down, Right, Down, Right, Right, then end Down and Left. Parachute, All Explosive Weapons and the Hauners H-AT all begin Right, Up, Left, Up, Right, Up, Left, Up, then end Right, Left and Up. No Fall Damage is the simplest of all: Down, seven Ups, then Down.

Practise without the game

Tap the sequences on your controller while it is off or on the home screen. Ten slow repetitions of an opener is usually enough for your thumb to learn it. The cheats database has a copy button for each code if you want the list on your phone beside you.

Do GTA 6 cheat codes follow a pattern?

The $250,000 code: a clockwise circle of the D-pad followed by a counter-clockwise one.

How long are GTA 6 cheat codes?

Every code is between seven and ten D-pad presses long.
