How to Memorise GTA 6 Cheat Codes Using Their Patterns

CheatsBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

GTA 6's D-pad codes look random until you line them up. Cheats in the same family share the same opening inputs and change only at the end. Learn four openers and a couple of shapes, and the 18 known codes become easy to keep in your head.

The ammo opener

Max Ammo, Infinite Ammo and No Reload all begin Down, Up, Right, Down, Left, Left, Up. Only the last three presses change. Max Ammo ends Up, Down, Left. Infinite Ammo ends Down, Down, Down. No Reload ends Down, Down, Up. Memory hook: Infinite keeps going down forever, and No Reload bounces back up.

The force opener

Attack Force, Exploding Balls and Vehicle Force all begin Down, Up, Left, Left, Right, Down. Attack Force ends Down, Up, Down. Exploding Balls is the same but its final press is Right. Vehicle Force swaps the seventh press to Left, giving Left, Up, Down.

The vehicle opener

Every known vehicle spawn begins Left, Up, Right, Down, Left, a clockwise circle that closes back on Left. The Boomazoom then ends Left, Up, Left. The MTL Pumper ends Right, Right, Up and the HVY Bulldozer Right, Right, Down. Memory hook: Up for the Pumper, Down for the Bulldozer, like a blade dropping to the ground.

The filter opener

Cataclysm, Dollhouse and Faded Glory all begin Down, Down, Up, Right, Right, Up, Left, Left. Only the final two presses differ: Right, Right for Cataclysm, Down, Right for Dollhouse and Left, Right for Faded Glory.

The shapes

The $250,000 code is two circles: clockwise (Up, Right, Down, Left) and then counter-clockwise (Up, Left, Down, Right). Unlimited Focus is a seesaw: Down, Up, Up, then Down, Up three times, ending with Right. Cycle Weather repeats Down, Right, Up twice and finishes with Right. The two wanted codes begin Left, Down, Right, Down, Up and split on the sixth press: Down for heat, Right for wanted.

Practise without the game

Tap the sequences on your controller while it is off or on the home screen. Ten slow repetitions of an opener is usually enough for your thumb to learn it. The cheats database has a copy button for each code if you want the list on your phone beside you.

Frequently asked questions

Do GTA 6 cheat codes follow a pattern?

Yes. Cheats in the same family share their opening inputs and differ only in the last two or three presses.

Which GTA 6 code is easiest to remember?

The $250,000 code: a clockwise circle of the D-pad followed by a counter-clockwise one.

How long are GTA 6 cheat codes?

The known codes run from seven to ten D-pad presses.
