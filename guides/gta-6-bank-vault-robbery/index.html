GTA 6 Bank Vault Robberies: Unlock, Method and Payout

MoneyBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

Bank vaults are the biggest repeatable score in GTA 6. They arrive late, they bring the police down hard, and they are tied to a Silver trophy. Details below come from launch-week guides, so treat exact figures as reported, not official.

When vaults unlock

Vault robberies open near the end of Chapter 4 through an optional mission. Because it is optional, it is possible to finish the chapter without it, so look for it before you move on if you want the trophy.

How a vault job works

The reported method relies on the armored vans that service banks.

  1. Follow an armored van to a bank, or wait at a bank until one arrives.
  2. When the guard carries money inside, the vault is open. Follow the guard in while it is.
  3. Take the cash and leave. Expect an immediate, heavy wanted level.
  4. Escape on something fast, then switch vehicles to shake the description.
  5. Deliver the take to your fence to turn it into usable money.

Guides report that the fence, Yasmani, also sells tips on van routes. Jobs done for him earlier in Chapter 3 are reported to be a requirement for that service.

Payout

A single vault is reported to pay up to $205,000. For comparison, a jewelry store pays $10,000 to $25,000 and a Wish List supercar $30,000 or more. One vault is worth roughly eight to twenty jewelry stores.

Surviving the getaway

The getaway is the hard part. Park your escape vehicle before you start, facing the exit. A motorbike gets through traffic better than a car. Once you break line of sight, deal with what the police know about you: change vehicle, change clothes and holster your weapon. The wanted level guide explains each heat icon.

The Maximum Withdrawal trophy

Robbing five bank vaults and escaping each time earns Maximum Withdrawal, a Silver trophy worth 40 Gamerscore on Xbox. It is one of six Silver trophies in the GTA 6 trophy list.
