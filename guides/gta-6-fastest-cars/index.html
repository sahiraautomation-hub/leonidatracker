The Fastest Cars in GTA 6, Ranked by In-Game Speed

VehiclesBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

The fastest car in GTA 6 is the Truffade Thrax. It has a stock speed rating of 8.7 out of 10, ahead of every other car players have measured since launch. This ranking uses the speed stat the game itself shows for each vehicle, because that is the one number several sources agree on.

The top seven

Supercars take five of the seven places. The two exceptions are worth noting: the Bravado Gauntlet Hellfire is a muscle car that ties for fourth, and the Pfister Comet S2 leads the sports class.

Why not miles per hour?

You will see lists online that rank GTA 6 cars by top speed in mph and put a different car first. Most of those were published before launch and are estimates. The in-game stat is what you can check for yourself in the vehicle menu, so that is what this page uses. If measured top speeds are confirmed later, they will be added.

How to get the Thrax and Emerus

The two fastest cars are both rewards in the cheat system. Completing every Shopping challenge unlocks the Truffade Thrax spawn cheat, and completing every Vehicle challenge unlocks the Progen Emerus spawn cheat. Both are covered in the challenges guide. Guides also report that the Thrax is the reward for finishing Randy's Wish List, and that the Emerus appears as a rare spawn in wealthy parts of Vice City.

Stealing supercars

High-end cars cannot simply be driven away. You need the Key Cloner, which arrives midway through Chapter 3. Players report that mansion driveways and garages in the wealthy districts are the best places to look. Scan a car with WAiNK first to check for a tracker.

Best all-rounder

Raw speed is not everything. The Pfister Comet S2 pairs its 7.7 speed with 8.0 acceleration and 7.6 handling, which makes it easier to drive quickly through traffic than a faster supercar. It is reported to be offered to Jason during a Chapter 4 story mission, so you do not need to steal one.

Keeping a fast car

A stolen car is lost unless you register it. Once you find something quick, follow the guide to keeping cars and give it a slot in one of your garages.
