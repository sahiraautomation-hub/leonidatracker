GTA 6 Fast Travel and How to Switch Between Jason and Lucia

BasicsBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

Leonida is big, and GTA 6 does not have a fast travel menu. It does have a paid shortcut, and from Chapter 2 you can jump between its two lead characters. Here is how both work.

How to fast travel

  1. Take out your phone with Up on the D-pad.
  2. Call a cab.
  3. Get in and choose your destination.
  4. Pay the extra fee to skip the journey. It is widely reported as $100.

That is the only fast travel in the game. There is no map-based teleport.

Fast travel limits

A taxi has to be able to reach you. Players report that cabs may not come to small islands, so head for the mainland or a bridge before you call. You also need the fare, so keep a little cash in hand or in your bank account.

Getting your own car back

Often you do not need a taxi at all, just your car. In Chapter 1, call Joey from your contacts to have Jason's car brought back. From Chapter 2, the Scooter Bros app delivers any registered vehicle to you for a fee. See keeping cars.

How to switch characters

Switching between Jason and Lucia unlocks after the Chapter 2 mission Breakfast Run. Hold Right on the D-pad to open the partner menu, then press R1 on PS5 or RB on Xbox.

Why switching matters

  • Each character has separate stats and a separate Criminal Profile.
  • Some side missions belong to one character. Andy's strand is for Lucia, while Tyrone's and Bret's are for Jason. See Strangers and Freaks.
  • Splitting from your partner is also one way to clear a Heat icon when the police are looking for a pair.

The partner menu

Holding Right on the D-pad does more than switch. The same menu lets you talk to your partner and tell them to split up or take the car, which is useful during an escape. Time and activities with your partner also count toward several cheats. See partner cheats.

Frequently asked questions

Is there fast travel in GTA 6?

Yes, by taxi. Call a cab from your phone and pay an extra fee, widely reported as $100, to skip the ride.

How do you switch between Jason and Lucia?

Hold Right on the D-pad and press R1 on PS5 or RB on Xbox. This unlocks after the Chapter 2 mission Breakfast Run.

When can you switch characters in GTA 6?

After Breakfast Run in Chapter 2.
