GTA 6 Cheats for Xbox Series X|S: Codes with LB and RB Controls

CheatsBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

Xbox Series X and Series S use the same GTA 6 cheat codes as PS5, with LB and RB in place of L1 and R1. All 61 codes below show the Xbox input.

Xbox input

  • Press Up on the D-pad to take out the phone.
  • Hold LB and press RB.
  • Keep holding and enter the D-pad sequence.
  • Look for the on-screen confirmation.

Press each direction cleanly and avoid rolling your thumb onto a diagonal between inputs.
