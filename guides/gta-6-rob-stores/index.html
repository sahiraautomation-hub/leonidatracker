How to Rob Stores in GTA 6 Without Getting Caught

MoneyBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

Quick answer: aim at the staff and threaten them so they empty the register. Then go to the back room and switch off the alarm, because the alarm is what calls the police. Open the back-room safe, destroy the camera footage and bank the cash. Each step is explained below.

Pick a target on the map

Open the map and look under Robberies. Stores are listed there with two numbers that matter: a Security level of Low, Medium or High, and a Take Value. Start with Low security stores. High security places are reported to have armed guards. You can also rob stores that are not flagged on the map. If you can walk into it, you can hold it up.

The clean method

  1. Control the room. Draw your weapon and aim at the staff, then use Threaten so they empty the register. Make sure they stay put. Staff who are left alone can run, fight back or raise the alarm.
  2. Disable the alarm. The security system is switched off from the back room. The alarm is what calls the police, so this is the step that buys you time.
  3. Open the safe. The back room usually has a safe that holds far more than the register. The code comes from inside the store, either a note in the back room or the staff.
  4. Destroy the footage. Cameras do not call the police, but they record your face. Find the security computer and destroy the recordings so you cannot be identified later.
  5. Leave by the back if there is an exit, and get clear of the area.

Going in quietly

Kicking in a back door sets off the alarm. Later in the game the Snap Gun, a lock-picking tool sold by a fence in Vice City, opens doors silently. With it you can reach the back room before anyone knows you are there. It is essential for jewelry stores.

After the robbery

Police share your description over the radio. Change clothes, swap or respray your vehicle and holster your weapon to clear what they know. The wanted level guide covers each icon. Then bank the cash, because you lose whatever you are carrying if you are caught. See keeping money safe.

Trophies and cheats tied to stores

  • Retail Therapy (Bronze): rob three shops in a row with your partner without losing your wanted level.
  • Faded Glory cheat: rob 50 stores with your partner. Log your count in the Tracker.
  • How violently you rob also moves your Criminal Profile. Knocking staff out instead of shooting keeps it clean.

Frequently asked questions

How do you rob a store in GTA 6?

Aim your weapon at the staff and use Threaten to empty the register. For a clean job, also disable the alarm in the back room, open the safe and destroy the security footage.

How do I stop the police being called?

Disable the security system from the back room and keep the staff under control. The alarm is what alerts the police.

Can you rob any store in GTA 6?

Yes. Any business you can walk into can be held up, not only the ones listed on the map.
