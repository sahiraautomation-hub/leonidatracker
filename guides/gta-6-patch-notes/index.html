GTA 6 Patch Notes and Update Tracker

BasicsBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·3 min read

Rockstar released the first GTA 6 update since launch on 23 November 2026. This page lists each update, what Rockstar says it changed and what the notes leave out. It was last checked on 24 November 2026 and will be updated when a new patch ships.

Latest update: 01.002.002

The update came out on 23 November 2026, four days after launch, for both PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. On PS5 it appears as version 01.002.002. On Xbox the same update is reported as version 1.0.2.2.

The download is small. It is reported at about 604 MB on PS5 and about 1.6 GB on Xbox. Those sizes come from update trackers and press coverage, not from Rockstar, so treat them as reported figures.

What update 01.002.002 fixed

  • Several problems that made the game crash.
  • Weather that stayed the same for long stretches.
  • Autosave not saving correctly.
  • Phone calls that failed to start, including calls that move the story on or open up missions.
  • Players being unable to lose a wanted level.
  • The deadlift bar at the gym not working.
  • Partners not handing over bags, which stopped you from selling them to a fence.

Rockstar published the notes on its support site later on release day. The first news reports went out before that, which is why some early articles say there were no notes. The list has seven fixes under one general heading. Rockstar gives no further detail on any of them. It does not say which crashes were fixed, for example.

The autosave fix is the one most players will care about. Coverage of the patch reported that some players had lost progress to the bug. Autosave is still no replacement for a manual save, so keep the routine from the saving guide.

If you were stuck with a wanted level that would not clear, that bug is on the list. The normal ways to lose the police are in the wanted level and Heat guide. The deadlift bar is part of weight training, which is how you raise Strength. The stats guide explains how that works.

What the notes do not mention

None of the seven fixes mentions cheats, prices or payouts. The cheat codes on this site were re-checked on 24 November 2026, after the patch, and the 21 known codes still match what other outlets list. See the full cheats list for the current state.

The notes are short, and they name only the seven items above. If a problem you have is not on the list, assume it is still open until Rockstar says otherwise. We will not list a fix here unless the published notes include it.

How the rollout worked

On PS5 the update did not reach everyone at once. Trackers reported three stages: about a third of players at first, about two thirds three hours later, and everyone after about six hours. Rockstar did not announce the staging itself. Sources differ on whether the stages went by region or by share of players.

No staging was reported for Xbox.

How to get the update

Both consoles normally download game updates on their own when they are online. If yours has not, you can ask for it by hand.

  1. On PS5, highlight GTA 6 on the home screen, press the Options button and choose Check for Update.
  2. On Xbox Series X|S, open My games & apps, choose Manage, then Updates.

Game updates do not affect your Tracker progress, which is stored in your browser.

Earlier updates

Two builds were reported before launch, during the pre-load period: 01.002.000 on 13 November 2026 and 01.002.001 on 16 November 2026. Rockstar published no notes for either, so there is nothing confirmed to list for them. The release date guide covers the pre-load dates.
