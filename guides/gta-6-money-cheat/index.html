GTA 6 Money Cheat: $250,000 Code and How to Unlock It

CheatsBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

GTA 6 has exactly one money cheat, and it pays out $250,000 at a time. It is one of the four cheats that unlock as soon as the story ends, so it is usually the first cheat players try.

The code

Take out your phone with Up on the D-pad, hold L1 and press R1 on PS5 (LB and RB on Xbox), then enter Up, Right, Down, Left, Up, Left, Down, Right. The sequence is easy to remember as a full clockwise turn of the D-pad followed by a reversed, counter-clockwise one.

When it unlocks

The only requirement is finishing the main story. There is no separate challenge for this cheat, unlike most of the other 60.

The catch: no saving, no trophies

Activating any cheat disables saving, trophies and achievements for that session. That makes the money cheat poor for long-term progress but useful for testing. Load a backup save, enter the code a few times, and try out the properties, vehicles or outfits you are weighing up. Once you know what you want, reload your clean save and earn the cash properly.

Legit ways to fund 100%

If the goal is completion, money from activities you need anyway is worth more than cheat cash you cannot save. Selling collectibles at pawn shops counts toward the Pixel Art cheat ($250,000 total), and selling vehicles counts toward Dark Mirror ($500,000 total). Track both totals in the Tracker.

Earning money without cheats

The cheat only arrives after the credits. For the 91 missions before that, see how to make money fast in every chapter, which covers car sales, jewelry stores and bank vaults.

Cheat-by-cheat notes

$250,000

Adds $250,000 to your balance instantly. To unlock it, finish the main story. The input is 8 presses: Up, Right, Down, Left, Up, Left, Down, Right. Use it on a throwaway save to test-drive expensive purchases before committing real story money.

Frequently asked questions

Can I enter the money cheat more than once?

Each entry triggers the cheat again, but the session will not save, so the money disappears when you reload a clean save.

Is there an unlimited money cheat in GTA 6?

No. The only money cheat among the 61 gives $250,000 per use.

Does the money cheat work in GTA Online?

Cheats are a Story Mode feature.
