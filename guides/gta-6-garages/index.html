GTA 6 Garages: All 8 You Can Buy, Prices and How to Unlock Them

VehiclesBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

Garages are the only property you can buy in GTA 6 at launch. There are eight for sale, each holding one car, and owning all of them earns a trophy. You cannot buy homes or businesses.

How to unlock garage buying

Garages are sold through the Vice City Real Estate app on your phone. The app arrives shortly after the Chapter 2 mission Daily Driver. Open it, pick a garage and pay from your bank balance.

What they cost

The eight garages are widely reported at the prices below, for a total of $160,000. One source reports these figures already include a 20% discount that applies once your BuckMe savings pass $100,000, which would put the undiscounted total at $200,000. If the app shows you higher prices, that is the likely reason.

Which to buy first

Mitchell Auto Center is the cheapest at $4,000 and sits near Port Gellhorn, where Chapter 2 takes place, so it is the natural first purchase. After that, 7th Avenue is the cheapest garage inside Vice City and is useful from Chapter 3 onward.

Free garages

You also receive garages without paying. The story hands over several in Vice City during the later chapters. Others come with editions and memberships: the Ultimate Edition, the Vintage Vice City pre-order pack and GTA+ each include one. None of these count toward the trophy.

The Little Boxes trophy

Buying all eight garages from the app earns Little Boxes, a Bronze trophy. Money spent here also counts toward Treat Yo Self, which asks you to spend $100,000 across all shops. Both are in the trophy list.

Filling your garages

A garage is only useful with a registered car in it. The guide to keeping cars explains registration, and the fastest cars list helps you decide what deserves a slot.

Frequently asked questions

How many garages can you buy in GTA 6?

Eight, through the Vice City Real Estate app. Each holds one car.

How much do all the garages cost in GTA 6?

About $160,000 in total at the widely reported prices, from $4,000 to $40,000 each.

Can you buy houses or businesses in GTA 6?

Not at launch. Garages are the only property for sale.
