GTA 6 Map: Every Region of Leonida and How Big It Is

WorldBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

GTA 6 is set in the state of Leonida, Rockstar's take on Florida. The map is divided into six named regions, with Vice City at its centre.

The six regions

Vice City is the largest city and the hub of the map. The Leonida Keys are the island chain where the story opens. Grassrivers is wetland country, Port Gellhorn is a coastal port region, Ambrosia sits inland, and Mount Kalaga National Park covers the wild north. Each one has its own collectibles, so exploring region by region is the cleanest way to work toward 100%.

How big is the map?

Rockstar has described Leonida as about twice the size of GTA 5's map. It has not published an official square-kilometre figure, so treat any exact number you see elsewhere as an estimate.

The official printable map

Rockstar announced a free printable Leonida map and cover-art poster for launch day as a digital download on its official GTA VI site, with a physical poster sold separately.
