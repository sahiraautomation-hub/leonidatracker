GTA 6 Collectible Cards: 18 Sets, 480 Cards and What Counts for 100%

CollectiblesBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

Collectible cards are the biggest collectible in GTA 6: 480 cards spread across 18 sets. You do not need all of them, though. Fifteen complete sets count toward 100%.

The numbers

  • 18 sets and 480 cards in total.
  • 15 complete sets count for 100% completion.
  • Completing any three sets earns the silver Don't Touch My Deck trophy.
  • Because set sizes differ, 15 sets take at least 372 cards, depending on which you choose.

Known set sizes

Five sets are the largest at 36 cards: Jack of Hearts Dancers, Nine1Nine Dancers, The Legends of Ratface, Right Wing: America's Hero and Ed Comics Universe. Vice City Club De Fútbol has 30. Vice City Flamingos, Vice City Narcos and Vice City Manatees have 24 each. Delights Dancers has 18, and Backyard Wrestling is the smallest at 12.

Which sets to prioritise

Finish the Vice City Flamingos set early, because all 24 cards unlock the Bubblegum filter cheat. For 100%, the smart move is to skip three of the 36-card sets and finish the smaller ones. Cards you do not need can be sold at pawn shops, which also counts toward the $250,000 collectible-sales total for the Pixel Art cheat.

Where cards turn up

Cards sit at fixed spots around Leonida, on surfaces like tables, toolboxes and garage shelves. Keep a running count in the Tracker, and note each completed set separately.
