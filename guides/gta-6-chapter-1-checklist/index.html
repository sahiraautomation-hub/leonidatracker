GTA 6 Chapter 1 Checklist: Things to Do Before Tourist Trap

ProgressBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·5 min read

Quick answer: before you start Tourist Trap, make a manual save, empty the floorboard stash, put $5,000 into Family Savings and spend the rest on guns and clothes. Do Shirley's two errands and the balcony meditation with Lucia if you want to see them, because they are gone afterwards. Jason's house repairs and errands have an earlier deadline: finish them before the mission Coming Home. Nothing on this page spoils the story.

When Chapter 1 locks

Tourist Trap is the point of no return. It runs straight into Home Sweet Home and Discharge Time, the last two missions of the chapter, and guides report no free roam again until Chapter 2 has begun. GTA 6 has no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so an older save is the only way back.

Guides differ on exactly how Tourist Trap begins once the mission before it, Recalibration, is done. One says it starts on its own, and others describe a marker or a pick-up that you can delay. The safe plan is to finish this checklist and make your save before Recalibration. The Chapter 1 mission list shows where both sit in the order.

Sort out your money first

This is the step that costs players the most. Guides report that when Chapter 1 ends you lose the cash you are carrying, the balance in your BuckMe checking account and anything under Jason's floorboards. Moving stash money into the bank is not enough on its own.

  1. Empty the floorboard stash in Jason's bedroom.
  2. Put $5,000 into Family Savings at an ATM. It is the savings side of the BuckMe app, which opens with the mission Buck Me, and guides report that it carries over. Two guides suggest $5,000. Guides describe Family Savings as a savings goal, not spending money, so do not put in more than you mean to.
  3. Spend the rest on guns and clothes. Guides report that bought weapons, their attachments and your clothes are all kept.
  4. Leave Jason's car alone. The car and its upgrades are reported not to follow you into Chapter 2.

The money safety guide explains the stash and the bank in more detail.

Chapter 1-only extras

  • Get Shirley's Rum and Steal the Scooter. Two errands for Jason's neighbour, Shirley. The rum comes from Three Sheets Liquor, and the scooter errand is reported to open about a day after the first one.
  • Meditate with Lucia. An optional meditation on the balcony of Jason's house, on the morning after the mission Down to Earth. Guides report that it does not count toward the Positive Mental Attitude trophy.
  • Clock Out. Lucia calls after the mission Day Release. Guides list this one as time-limited, and they say to collect her before 4 pm.
  • Ring the Bell! Cal texts Jason to meet at the Rusty Anchor. Two guides say to do it before the mission Swamped and one says it stays open afterwards, so do it early. It is reported to unlock a Rusty Anchor T-shirt and cap.
  • Ultimate Edition extras. The bonus bike, kayak and custom car build are reported to be lost when the chapter ends, so use them now.
  • Small pickups inside missions. Two guides point out cash in a trailer during Gone Fishing and a pair of sunglasses for Lucia during Day Release.

All of these are optional. One detailed guide reports that nothing in Chapter 1 is needed for a trophy or for 100%, so skip any that do not interest you.

The earlier deadline: before Coming Home

A second, earlier deadline catches people out. Jason can fix up the house and run a few errands before Lucia comes home, and guides report that these close with the mission Coming Home, well before Tourist Trap.

House repairs. Guides list the steps, the leaky roof, the railing and a wall that needs fixing and painting. They open as soon as free roam does, after Gone Fishing, and are reported to improve how things start between Jason and Lucia.

Errands after Feng Shui. Guides list groceries, flowers, a haircut, collecting Lucia's clothes and buying her a necklace. The necklace is reported to cost $475 at The Siren's Chest in south-west Key Lento.

What can wait

Most of what you find in the Keys is not missable. Guides report that Kenton's treasure dives, fishing with Cal and the gang hideouts pause when the chapter ends and come back later. Collectibles, challenges and cheat unlocks stay open after the credits, as the missables guide explains. Guides also report that you can return to the Keys during Chapter 2, though not straight away.

So there is no need to grind money or clear the map before Tourist Trap. Guides advise against farming cash in Chapter 1, since most of it is wiped.

How long Chapter 1 takes

Guides put Chapter 1 at roughly six to ten hours, depending on how much side content you do. One reviewer who did everything reports about 18 hours. The chapter has 22 main missions, and one guide counts 34 missions and tasks once the optional ones are included. Tick them off in the Tracker as you go.

The order to do it in

  1. After Gone Fishing, start the house repairs and make a manual save. See how to save.
  2. Do Shirley's rum errand, then the scooter errand when it opens.
  3. After Feng Shui, run Jason's errands and buy the necklace before Coming Home.
  4. After Down to Earth, meditate with Lucia on the balcony.
  5. After Day Release, answer Lucia's call for Clock Out, and meet Cal before Swamped.
  6. Before Recalibration, make a save you will not overwrite, empty the stash, fill Family Savings and spend the rest.
  7. Start Tourist Trap when you are ready for Chapter 2.

Frequently asked questions

What should I do before Tourist Trap in GTA 6?

Make a manual save, empty the floorboard stash, put $5,000 into Family Savings and spend the rest on guns and clothes. Do Shirley's two errands and the balcony meditation first if you want to see them.

Do you keep your money after Chapter 1 in GTA 6?

Guides report that only Family Savings carries over. Cash you are carrying, the BuckMe checking balance and the floorboard stash are all lost.

Do you keep your guns after Chapter 1 in GTA 6?

Guides report that guns you bought are kept, along with attachments and clothes. You collect the guns from an Ammu-Nation weapons locker in Chapter 2.

How long is Chapter 1 of GTA 6?

Guides put it at roughly six to ten hours. It has 22 main missions.
