GTA 6 Chapter 1 Missions: Just Like Paradise (All 22 in Order)

MissionsBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

Chapter 1 of GTA 6, Just Like Paradise, runs for 22 main story missions set around the Leonida Keys. It introduces most of the systems you will use for the rest of the game. This list stays spoiler-light: names and unlocks only.

What the chapter unlocks

  • Gear Up unlocks gun shops and loadout management.
  • Lab Fees introduces Brian's Lockup, the drop-off for stolen goods.
  • Unwanted Attention unlocks Pay 'n' Spray shops and the cop scanner.
  • Buck Me sets up your bank account.

The Prologue is played as Lucia, and Gone Fishing then switches to Jason. Tourist Trap is the point of no return: once you start it, Chapter 1-only content is gone, including Shirley's two errands and Lucia's balcony meditation.

Pacing tips

Chapter 1 is the best time to note activities you want to come back to. Gun shops open after Gear Up, and Pay 'n' Spray after Unwanted Attention, so wanted-level escapes get easier from that point.

Chapter 1 in numbers

Chapter 1 holds 22 of GTA 6's 91 story missions, about a quarter of the main story. Four of those 22 open systems you will use all game: gun shops, the lockup drop-off, Pay 'n' Spray with the cop scanner, and your bank account.

Before you finish the chapter

Some content exists only in Chapter 1. Work through the Chapter 1 checklist and the missables guide before Tourist Trap, and read the beginner tips if you are still in your first hours.
