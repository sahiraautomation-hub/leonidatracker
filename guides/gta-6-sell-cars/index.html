How to Sell Cars in GTA 6: Every Buyer and What They Pay

MoneyBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

Selling stolen cars is the first dependable way to earn money in GTA 6. There are three buyers, each unlocked at a different point in the story, and each with its own rule about which cars it accepts. The key to all three is the tracker.

Check the tracker first

Before you steal a car to sell, scan it with the WAiNK app. Hold Up on the D-pad to open it. The scan shows whether the car has a tracker, its sell value and its Cost to Register. Which buyer will take the car depends on that tracker reading, so a five-second scan saves a wasted drive.

Moses: Chapter 2

Moses runs a garage in Port Gellhorn and becomes available after the Chapter 2 story mission named after him. He pays $1,000 or more per car and is the best early source of steady money.

Moses only accepts cars without a tracker. Guides also report two limits: he will not take a car stolen right outside his own garage, and sales are rate-limited, with a short cooldown between cars and a cap on how many he buys per day. Sleeping resets the daily cap.

Pay 'n' Spray: Chapter 3

From early Chapter 3, any Pay 'n' Spray buys cars that carry a Standard Tracker, which are exactly the cars Moses refuses. A good car sells for around $4,000. Because Pay 'n' Spray shops are spread across the map, this is also more convenient than driving back to Port Gellhorn.

Randy's Wish List: mid Chapter 3

Randy wants specific high-end sports cars and pays $30,000 or more for each one delivered in perfect condition. You need the Key Cloner to steal them, which you receive in the mission that introduces Randy. This is the best car money in the game. See Randy's Wish List.

Selling or keeping?

The same shops that buy cars can also register them so you keep them permanently. If a car is rare or fast, compare its sell value with its Cost to Register in WAiNK before deciding. The guide to keeping cars covers registration, and selling vehicles also counts toward the $500,000 total that unlocks the Dark Mirror cheat.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I sell cars in GTA 6?

Moses in Port Gellhorn from Chapter 2, any Pay 'n' Spray from Chapter 3, and Randy for the high-end cars on his Wish List.

Why won't Moses buy my car?

Moses only takes cars without a tracker. Scan the car with WAiNK first. Tracked cars can be sold at Pay 'n' Spray from Chapter 3.

Can I sell cars in Chapter 1?

No. The first car buyer, Moses, unlocks in Chapter 2.
