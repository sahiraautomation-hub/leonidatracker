GTA 6 Fishing and Meditation: Unlocking Fisheye and Fast Forward

CollectiblesBy LeonidaTracker Editorial·Updated 2026-11-22·2 min read

Two of GTA 6's calmest activities hide two filter cheats. Catching 100 fish unlocks Fisheye, and completing 50 meditations unlocks Fast Forward.

Meditation

There are 18 meditation spots in Leonida. Fast Forward needs 50 meditations in total, so you will repeat spots rather than find 50 different ones. Pick the spots closest to where you play most.

Fishing

Fisheye needs 100 fish. Fishing works well as a break between story chapters, and a few catches at a time add up quickly.

Tracking both

The Tracker has separate counters for meditation spots found, meditations completed and fish caught.

A realistic pace

Fifty meditations across 18 spots works out to about three visits per spot if you rotate through all of them, or many more visits to the two or three spots nearest where you usually play. For fishing, catching five fish per session gets you to 100 in 20 sessions. Plug your own numbers into the play-time estimator to see how long either goal will take.

Why these two are good filler

Both goals are low-pressure and can be done in short bursts between missions, so they suit sessions when you only have a few minutes. The rewards are screen filters rather than gameplay changes, which makes them worth unlocking for photo mode once the bigger goals are done.
