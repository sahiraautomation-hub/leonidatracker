How to use the tracker

Checkboxes are for one-off milestones such as story missions, regions and race venues. Counters are for anything you collect or repeat: use the plus and minus buttons, or tap the number and type your exact total from the in-game Progress menu. Dollar counters step in hundreds or thousands so you can match big totals quickly.

Mission names are blurred by default so you can track the story without reading ahead. Tap a blurred name to reveal just that one, or untick Blur spoilers to show all of them.

Challenge counters link straight to the Cheat Unlock Planner and the progress bars on the cheats database, so updating them here shows you exactly which cheat is next.
