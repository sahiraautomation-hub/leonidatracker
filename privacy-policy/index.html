Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-22

This policy explains what information LeonidaTracker (https://theleonidatracker.com) handles and the choices you have.

Your tracker progress

Checklist ticks, counters, favourites and preferences are stored in your browser's local storage on your own device. They are never sent to our servers. We do not offer accounts and do not collect names, emails or passwords through the tools. Save codes you export are generated in your browser and stay with you. Clearing your browser's site data deletes this information.

This site may display ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.

  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google's My Ad Center.
  • You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • You can opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent message, provided through Google's certified consent management tools, before personalised ads or advertising cookies are used.

Server logs

Like any website, our hosting provider may record standard technical information such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and to keep the site running. We do not use these logs to identify individual visitors.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated.
