Picking a sensible minutes-per-item value

The estimator is only as good as its per-item time. A quick way to calibrate it: time yourself on your next three collectibles or challenge runs, including travel, and use the average. Strict lists such as underwater discoveries usually take longer per item than counters you advance during normal play, so run the estimator per category for a more honest number.

Frequently asked questions

How is completion calculated?

Each checkbox counts as 0 or 1. Each counter counts as your value divided by its target, capped at 1. A category is the average of its items, and overall is the average of the categories.

Why are some cheats missing from the planner?

Cheats that need every challenge on a track have no fixed number to measure, so they are listed on the cheats page instead.

Does this match the in-game percentage?

No. The in-game 100% uses Rockstar's own checklist. These tools measure the items you track here, and the 100% gap table uses the known in-game requirements.
